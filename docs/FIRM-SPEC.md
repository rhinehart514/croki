# Drover product specification

This document defines Drover's durable product and build physics. `docs/STATE.md` records what the
current tree proves. `DESIGN.md` defines the intended desktop experience. Translations cannot override
this file.

## Outcome

Drover is the Product and GTM Development Environment where one founder moves faster than the market
can react.

The founder continuously changes the Product and every path to market while Claude, Codex, and
specialized agents pursue ambitious work, try several approaches, do work that does not scale, learn
from reality, and make the next move faster.

Speed and improving velocity are the moat. Safety, provenance, and founder authority make that speed
usable; they are not the headline.

## Product promise

Drover lets one founder run Product and go-to-market as one fast, changing system. Direct Claude and
Codex, pursue several routes to growth, change the Product when reality demands it, and compound every
useful result into faster next moves.

The value order is:

1. move across Product and GTM with exceptional speed;
2. let agents do ambitious, creative, exact work;
3. pursue several approaches without losing coherence;
4. let market reality change the Product immediately;
5. make every cycle improve the next one;
6. retain founder authority over what becomes true or crosses into the world.

## Product laws

### Product and GTM are one changing system

Product truth, promise, offer, pricing, audience, experience, capability, and business model may all
change. PLG, outbound, inbound, partnerships, founder-led sales, manual onboarding, bespoke customer
work, and unusual experiments may coexist. No single route is privileged.

Completed Product work raises its Product/GTM consequences. Returned market evidence raises the
Product or GTM consequences it supports without fabricating an interpretation.

### Founder direction starts work

No ambient agent invents unrelated work. One broad founder direction may authorize a continuing
portfolio of safe inward work. That authority is explicit, durable, scoped to its origin, visible, and
revocable. It may survive restart and wake on attributable evidence.

The scope never authorizes unrelated work, destructive action, Product-truth promotion, capability
expansion, or an outward effect.

### Native agents remain native

The selected Claude or Codex SDK model is the agent the founder talks to. Drover supplies venture
context, durable Threads and Runs, isolated work, provisional Product models, evidence, review, and
authority. It does not insert a persona, automatic router, mandatory plan, or workflow language that
reduces the provider's native capability.

The SDK decides how to investigate, collaborate, branch, and build within the granted scope.

### Canonical truth is singular; interpretation may be plural

One venture store contains one adopted current Product/GTM model and any number of durable provisional
model branches. Agents may create and revise source-bearing provisional alternatives. Only the founder
may selectively merge them into current truth.

Current truth never changes merely because an agent proposed, inferred, rendered, or placed something.
Layout is not semantic truth.

### Facts, evidence, and interpretation remain separate

Repository facts, exact work, outward execution receipts, and returned evidence retain their source
identity. Agent interpretations stay tentative and source-bearing. Founder assertions stay attributable.
A missing link remains visible; Drover never fills it with plausible prose.

### Authority follows consequence

Safe inward work may continue under an exact founder scope. Anything crossing into the world waits for
the founder at the exact point of action. The founder alone may:

- promote Product/GTM truth;
- widen or revoke continuing authority;
- grant a capability;
- execute or authorize an outward action;
- perform destructive work;
- end founder-owned active work.

An agent may prepare exact outward material and expected return. Preparation never equals execution.

### Doing things that do not scale is first-class

Manual onboarding, founder interviews, bespoke customer work, hand-built outreach, and other
unrepeatable acts are valid work. Repetition may eventually earn a reusable mechanism. Drover never
requires repeatability before useful work can exist.

### Vocabulary is earned

- **Live work** is a derived view of everything working, waiting, returned, or needing judgment.
- **Motion** is a broad route to value or growth such as PLG, outbound, inbound, or partnerships.
- **Branch** is a causal alternative or provisional Product/GTM model.
- **Mechanism** is reusable capacity earned through repetition.
- **Campaign** is an optional bounded market activation.
- **Pipeline** is a literal throughput pipeline with known stages.
- **Release** is a Product release.
- **Outward action** is any exact act that crosses into the world.
- **Evidence** is what reality returned.
- **Thread** is durable founder conversation and direction.
- **Run** is one exact agent attempt.

There is no universal Mission, Front, Initiative, Bet, Program, Pipeline, or other work container.
Historical identifiers may remain inside compatibility adapters only.

## Canonical venture model

The schema-v3 canonical model contains:

- open semantic objects and relationships;
- Threads and Runs;
- durable provisional `ModelBranch` records;
- source-bearing `ModelChange` overlays;
- founder-only `ModelMergeReceipt` records;
- direction-scoped `WorkScope` authority;
- generic `OutwardActionReceipt` records;
- work, artifacts, evidence, interpretations, and founder decisions by exact reference.

Object type is an open string. Territory is Product, GTM, both, or unset. Motion, campaign, mechanism,
audience, offer, capability, experience, feature, release, and evidence are earned facets, not a closed role enum.
A campaign does not require a primary motion. A motion does not require a repeatability claim.

### Provisional Product models

A branch overlays current truth; it does not copy the venture. Each change names its target, operation,
base digest, rationale, sources, proposer, and supersession. Current changes beneath the branch create a
visible conflict. Independent writes retry after unrelated store contention. Conflicting writes to the
same record require founder resolution.

Product/GTM conversation may project the local branch above its Work composer as a correctable visual view.
The projection may contain current truth, proposals, alternatives, evidence, unknowns, actions, gates, and
outcomes. It must not impose workflow order or causal edges that the underlying material has not earned. A
workflow is one possible projection of established mechanics, not the default artifact or adoption unit.
Corrections revise the same branch and projection; founder review remains selective over exact `ModelChange`
records.

Merge is transactional, selective, attributable, and founder-only. Merge does not close continuing
work. Closing a branch preserves its history.

### Work scopes and execution

A `WorkScope` carries its originating Thread and founder message, objective, subjects, Product-model
branches, allowed inward effects, evidence wake conditions, restart behavior, spend policy, and stop
conditions.

Provider execution uses one isolated session per Run and one isolated worktree per coding attempt.
Locks belong to exact resume identities, not to a participant. Spend reservations are atomic. Actual
concurrency follows the machine, provider, and budget; Drover exposes backpressure honestly and sets no
product-level count.

### Outward actions and evidence return

Product release, deploy, outbound message, publication, founder interview, demo, manual onboarding,
bespoke deliverable, partner action, and spend are distinct kinds of outward action. A founder interview
does not become a release.

Each action retains its Product/GTM context, prepared material, founder decision, executor receipt,
expected return, observation authority, returned evidence, and subsequent Product/GTM consequence.
Evidence returns to exact affected claims and work; similarity alone does not create a causal join.

## Founder experience

Drover ships as Electron desktop with two founder surfaces over one model.

### Work

Work is a full direct Claude/Codex development environment. Conversation holds intent and progress;
exact code, artifacts, comparisons, terminal, preview, tests, evidence, and decisions open beside it.
One direction may form several child Threads and Runs. The founder can compare, steer, stop, or continue
exact attempts. Deleting a non-root chat removes it from Work, stops its live Runs, and revokes continuing
work rooted there; exact Product truth, artifacts, and receipts remain because deleting conversation cannot
rewrite venture history. Completed implementation raises a provisional Product-model consequence.

### Product / GTM

Product / GTM is the founder's intent layer. Tools everywhere else show state — code, dashboards,
metrics — and hide what the founder is trying to do. This canvas exists so the founder can see the
product laid out, say what should be different, and have that direction reach the agents exactly.
Seeing the product is what makes directing it possible: the map improves the prompt.

The Product territory is the product itself, mapped as the pages a user walks through — landing,
signup, workspace, invite, share, settings, and whatever the real product contains. The map is a
proper map, left to right in walk order: entry pages at the left, the walk deepening rightward, with
page-to-page connections drawn only from links and redirects the code proves. Agents derive the
map from the actual code when a repository connects and keep it true as the code changes; the founder
corrects names, grouping, and visibility. A page the code cannot support is never fabricated, and
neither is a connection between pages.

Clicking a page is the refine moment. The node expands in place to what the page is now — an honest
summary or preview — plus prior founder direction and the thinking attached to it, above a plain
brain-dump input. Sending that input mints an exact Work Thread scoped to that piece. This
click-to-refine consequence is what keeps the map from being a static diagram; a product map without
it is a defect. Refinement is not hard-scoped to one entry point: the founder may direct from the Work
composer or from any node, and decides what matters for expressing intent.

Every page also carries one GTM action: join an existing market play, or — when none exists — have an
agent propose a strategy for getting that piece in front of people, which lands on the GTM territory
as a proposal the founder adopts or discards.

At rest the map is clean: page names only, no badges. Direction, live work, and returned evidence live
one click deep on the page they belong to. Assumptions, proposals, decisions, and evidence attach to
the page they are about; whole-product thinking attaches to the product itself. Free-floating strategy
bubbles do not share the Product territory with the map.

The canvas remains one shared causal model with natural alternatives. Product, shared Product/GTM
truth, and GTM remain legible as territories inside that graph rather than becoming separate canvases
or fixed lanes. Its focal causal path advances left to right; curved edges preserve the reading
direction across territory changes. A restrained spectrum appears only on a relationship that crosses
territory. Current truth and provisional alternatives cannot visually collapse into one.

Founder placement is presentation, never semantic truth. Nodes are draggable and persist their position.
Agents, tools, and sources may be dropped only where Drover can resolve an exact subject and consequence;
unsupported drops refuse visibly and persist nothing. Selecting a node expands its exact detail, work,
evidence, or founder decision in place instead of opening a detached side inspector.

The GTM territory is workflows, in two honest registers. A **drafted play** is an intended workflow —
sophisticated and ambitious, never a thin sketch — visibly marked as not yet real. An **established play**
earned its standing by actually running. A draft is never displayed as established; the register marking
replaces the old rule that unproven motions stay vague blobs, because invisible intent is the exact
clouding this canvas exists to remove.

Plays are authored talk-first: the founder brain-dumps the play in conversation (later, also from a
page's GTM action), an agent drafts the full sequence onto the canvas aware of what else is already
running, and then walks the founder through it step by step. Corrections land on the exact step; any
step can branch or be refined later without redrafting the play.

A long play renders at full length, always — thirty steps across weeks lay out left to right and the
founder scrolls. The length is information: the founder should feel how heavy a play is. Steps are not
compressed into summary chapters.

A running play keeps the same drawn shape and shows derived counts on its steps — "5 waiting on reply",
"2 need your approval" — with the actual people one click behind each count. Counts derive from real
state, receipts, and returns; they are never hand-maintained. Founder gates, outward actions, and
evidence return unfold on this same canvas. Product remains connected as a dependency and consequence
rather than being forced into operational stages.

Conversation is contextual and closable here; the canvas or review path owns the center. Substantive
work follows the exact returned Thread into Work.

### First run and return

First run is one consequential choice: the founder chooses a real Product codebase and Drover derives its
name, grounds it locally, and opens Work immediately. The ordinary Work composer then asks **What are you
trying to make true?** and exposes the connected Claude or Codex runtime without making either another setup
gate. No organization, workflow, ontology, pipeline, campaign, or blank plan is configured first.

On return, the existing Threads, paths, deltas, and gates answer what changed, what is moving, what
failed or went stale, what reality returned, what needs founder judgment, and which Product/GTM model
changed. There is no summary dashboard competing with the work.

## Visual and interaction physics

Drover feels like calm velocity: near-black, precise, dense, responsive, and alive only where causality
is active. DM Sans carries operating language; mono is reserved for machine material. Blue means direct
action and focus. Amber means founder authority. A restrained spectrum identifies a Product/GTM territory
crossing. Current truth is solid; provisional truth is explicitly textured; evidence uses
source-bearing return curves.

Motion shows causality. Live work moves briefly. A founder gate becomes still. Returned evidence travels
back to the exact affected Product delta. Reduced motion preserves the same meaning without transforms.

Nodes are compact, readable pills. Exact deltas, evidence, and decisions receive the larger review
surface. Generic cards, raw object counts, status-dot theater, glowing AI decoration, raw transport
errors, and tiny metadata are product defects.

## Compatibility and deletion

Historical `bet`, `fork`, `gtm-ide`, `channel` storage records, architecture adapters, releases, and
`~/.gtm-ide` may remain readable until migration proves their removal safe. They do not define current
product language or receive new writes.

The following current product shapes are forbidden:

- Signal → Pipeline → Campaign → Outcome as a universal loop;
- fixed pipeline lanes;
- release as the universal unit of market movement;
- Heat as the founder model of autonomy;
- founder-facing System, Architecture, Firm, Bet, Fork, Teammate, or Pipeline compatibility language;
- separate Releases or System workspaces;
- organization-board, Atlas, pipeline-builder, and release-manager visual grammars.

Compatibility cannot justify contradictory current UI.

## Verification

Mechanical readiness requires Brain tests, UI unit tests, lint, production builds, dead-code,
architecture, size, bundle, site, browser, Electron, package, and acceptance gates.

Product readiness additionally requires one real dogfood loop:

```text
connect repository
→ state what should become true
→ agents begin Product/GTM work
→ provisional Product model changes
→ exact outward action waits for founder
→ reality returns
→ Product delta updates
→ founder selectively merges
→ next work begins faster
```

Tests cannot establish product coherence by themselves. The shipped first run, Work, Product/GTM,
return experience, public promise, and compatibility deletion must tell the same truth.
