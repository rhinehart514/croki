# Drover features and value proposition

**Status:** current strategy translation, 2026-07-17. This document cannot add product physics or claim
implementation. [`FIRM-SPEC.md`](FIRM-SPEC.md) owns the contract; [`STATE.md`](STATE.md) owns proof.

## Strategic center

AI has made it cheap to produce work. It has not made a venture coherent. A solo founder still has to carry
Product truth across sessions, connect it to go-to-market, decide what may touch the world, notice what
reality returned, and teach that understanding to the next attempt.

Drover's core value is **whole-venture cognition under founder authority**:

> See the full Product and go-to-market system, manipulate it directly, direct Claude and Codex against the
> right context, and let evidence change what the venture does next without surrendering consequence.

The product is not valuable because it contains agents, a canvas, chat, worktrees, or an approval queue. It
is valuable when those mechanics give one founder a more complete mental model, stronger judgment, and
more ability to act.

## Primary user and job

The primary user is a technical solo founder who can already build with frontier coding agents. Their
constraint is maintaining a coherent Product-and-market system without operating many chats, branches,
automations, dashboards, synthetic employees, or taxonomies.

The core job is:

> Help me understand, change, reach the market with, and learn from my venture as one system while I retain
> the judgment and authority that only I should hold.

That job contains seven needs:

1. Begin from what the Product and market actually are, not a blank prompt.
2. See the whole venture and its missing or unsupported links.
3. Direct one object or the whole venture without reconstructing context.
4. Explore parallel approaches without designing a workflow graph first.
5. Inspect exact work, provenance, and verification where correction matters.
6. Hold every world-touching consequence at the founder boundary.
7. See returned evidence change Product and go-to-market understanding.

The emotional outcome is not productivity. It is **depth without loss of control**.

## Lead with these

- **The whole Product and go-to-market system, visible and manipulable.**
- **One selection-scoped conversation over one canonical venture.**
- **Bounded Claude/Codex execution with inspectable provenance.**
- **Exact founder authority over every consequence.**
- **Evidence that visibly changes understanding and next action.**

## Reasons to believe

- real repository binding and cited Product truth;
- isolated git worktrees and exact differences;
- venture-scoped local readable persistence;
- provider-neutral Claude Code and Codex runtimes;
- runtime/model/auth/configuration/cost receipts;
- short-lived host-signed founder authority;
- honest unattributed evidence;
- reversible semantic revisions and placement separated from truth.

These mechanics support the promise. They are not the headline.

## Do not mistake category infrastructure for differentiation

Chat, multiple models, agent identity, parallel tasks, background execution, worktrees, diff review,
activity history, schedules, and approval queues are expected. Drover's differentiated claim is the joined
system: Product and go-to-market truth, direct visual cognition, bounded execution, exact authority, returned
evidence, and durable founder judgment.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Strength | Unresolved job Drover targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coding agent | Changes software quickly. | Usually stops at code/session boundaries and does not preserve the market act, returned evidence, and changed venture understanding. |
| General AI chat | Reasons flexibly. | Durable truth, scope, artifacts, authority, and learning fragment across transcripts. |
| Agent manager / automation platform | Dispatches work and connects tools. | The founder becomes workflow designer or synthetic-team manager; activity outruns understanding. |
| CRM / GTM suite | Organizes campaigns, contacts, and metrics. | Product change and Product truth remain outside; the schema arrives before learning. |
| Project manager / review queue | Makes assigned work and status inspectable. | Records coordination without providing a manipulable causal venture model or evidence-return loop. |
| Static canvas | Supports spatial thinking. | Cannot execute, verify, preserve runtime provenance, or enforce consequence authority. |

Drover should win only when the founder values the whole system. A focused tool may be better for one code
change, campaign, or fixed automation.

## Alpha hypotheses

1. **Cognition:** the canvas gives a materially better mental model than chats, lists, or static diagrams.
2. **Specificity:** repository truth and accumulated evidence produce work native to the venture.
3. **Direction leverage:** selecting and directing an object is faster and more accurate than reprompting.
4. **Review leverage:** inspecting and refining staged work costs less than producing it from scratch.
5. **Authority trust:** the founder expands run freedom because the consequence boundary is exact and real.
6. **Learning continuity:** evidence changes the next decision while preserving the difference between fact,
   evidence, interpretation, and causality.

The decisive alpha scene is:

> A founder sees the whole venture, selects what matters, directs bounded Claude/Codex work, inspects an
> exact artifact and its proof, authorizes one consequence, receives evidence, and watches that evidence
> change the venture model and next action.
