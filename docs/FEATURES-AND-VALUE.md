# Drover features and value proposition

**Status:** current strategy translation, 2026-07-15. This document explains the value implied by
the current product contract; it does not add features or override
[`FIRM-SPEC.md`](FIRM-SPEC.md). [`STATE.md`](STATE.md) remains the authority for what is built and
what remains unproven.

## Strategic center

AI has made it cheap to produce more work. It has not made a venture coherent. A solo founder still
has to carry product truth across sessions, turn broad direction into specific work, reconcile code
and go-to-market, decide what may touch the world, notice what reality returned, and teach all of
that to the next attempt.

Drover's core value is **continuity under founder authority**:

> Give Drover a real product and a direction. It keeps product and market work moving as one
> evidence-backed loop, brings only consequential acts to the founder, and lets returned reality
> improve what happens next.

The product is not valuable because it contains agents, a canvas, chat, worktrees, or an approval
queue. It is valuable when those mechanisms combine into a venture that can keep working and
learning without becoming unsafe, generic, or burdensome to operate.

## Primary user and job

The primary user is a technical solo founder with one or more software products. They can already
make software quickly with frontier coding agents. Their constraint is not access to generation; it
is maintaining a coherent product-and-market operation without becoming the operator of many chats,
branches, automations, dashboards, and synthetic employees.

The core job is:

> Help my product find its market and keep moving while I retain the judgment and control that only
> I should hold.

That job contains six recurring needs:

1. Begin from what the product actually is, not from a blank prompt or generic playbook.
2. Turn one direction into multiple real attempts without manually decomposing and dispatching them.
3. Inspect, challenge, and refine work at the level where a correction matters.
4. Let safe inward work continue without granting ambient permission to act on the world.
5. Join real responses to the attempts that caused them and carry the learning forward.
6. Direct more than one venture without mixing their truth, work, or memory.

The strongest emotional outcome is not productivity. It is **momentum without loss of control**: the
founder can leave, return, understand what changed, and exercise judgment instead of reconstructing
context or managing activity.

## Value proposition stack

| Layer | Working articulation | What it must prove |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate wedge | **Vibe code your go-to-market.** Turn a real codebase and a broad direction into grounded product-and-market bets. | One outside founder reaches a useful real-world act faster than they would through disconnected coding and GTM tools. |
| Daily value | **Wake up to decisions, not work.** Drover stages useful work while the founder is away and compresses the return to evidence, exact decisions, and consequences. | Unattended work is worth reviewing, the queue stays bounded, and the founder can recover the state of the venture in minutes. |
| Trust promise | **Autonomy inside; the founder's hand at the wall.** Inward work may continue, but every send, publish, deploy, and spend waits for explicit founder authority. | The wall is technically non-forgeable and experientially trusted; review costs less than doing the work manually. |
| Learning value | **Every result changes the next attempt.** Positive, negative, and zero outcomes return to the bet and inform later judgment. | A real attributable outcome visibly changes a later fork without overstating causality. |
| Destination | **The operating system for one-person holding companies.** Multiple isolated, transferable ventures work and learn behind one founder wall. | A founder can direct two real ventures, move one between machines or owners, and reuse only deliberately blessed patterns. |

These layers should not be collapsed into one claim. The wedge explains why someone tries Drover
now. The destination explains why the same product can become much larger. The wall makes increasing
autonomy credible. The outcome loop makes continued use compound rather than merely accumulate
outputs.

screen layout.

## Feature hierarchy

### Lead with these

These are the product's differentiated capability, and the later UX should make their value
immediately legible:

- **One continuous product-market loop.** A product change, market action, returned response, and next
  change remain part of the same bet rather than separate tools and handoffs.
- **Useful work while the founder is away.** The firm advances reversible inward work and returns
  consequential differences instead of an activity feed.
- **Exact founder authority.** The founder sees the precise act, difference, evidence, and valid
  decision before anything touches the world.
- **Reality that compounds.** Returned outcomes and founder judgment change later work with visible
  attribution.
- **One firm, then one portfolio.** The same physics scales from one codebase to isolated ventures
  behind one wall.

### Use as reasons to believe

These mechanics make the core promise credible but should not become the headline by themselves:

- cited repository grounding;
- isolated git worktrees and exact differences;
- versioned, reversible firm configuration;
- real runtime, model, cost, and configuration provenance;
- local, readable venture records and explicit transfer;
- provider-neutral Claude Code and Codex execution;
- typed wall decisions, presence holds, and durable receipts;
- outcome identity, joins, and honest unattributed returns.

### Treat as surfaces, not value propositions

Conversation, the infinite canvas, participant faces, configuration differences, the outline, the
portfolio wall, MCP, and runtime selection are interaction or delivery surfaces. They are successful
only when they make the core loop easier to direct, understand, correct, trust, or continue.

The canvas in particular is not the product category. It is the living proof scene for the firm. If
it shows topology without consequence, it is decoration. If configuration becomes a visible catalog
of fields, it turns a flexible firm into administration. If named teammates require the founder to
manage a simulated org chart, they obscure rather than deliver continuity.

### Do not mistake table stakes for differentiation

Chat, multiple models, agent identity, parallel tasks, background execution, worktrees, diff review,
activity history, schedules, and approval queues are increasingly expected in agent products. Drover
can use all of them, but its differentiated claim is the joined system: durable product truth,
product-and-market work, exact authority, returned outcomes, learned taste, and venture ownership.

## Why Drover instead of the obvious alternatives

| Alternative | What it does well | The unresolved job Drover targets |
|---|---|---|
| General coding agent | Produces and changes code quickly. | It usually ends at the code or session boundary; it does not own the market act, returned outcome, and next venture decision as one loop. |
| General AI chat | Reasons flexibly from a prompt. | Product truth, work, permission, and learning fragment across transcripts and require founder reconstruction. |
| Agent manager or automation platform | Dispatches many tasks and connects tools. | The founder becomes a workflow designer, scheduler, or synthetic team manager, and activity can grow faster than useful decisions. |
| GTM suite or CRM | Organizes contacts, campaigns, funnels, and metrics. | Product change remains outside the system, and the tool imposes a work taxonomy before the venture has learned what matters. |
| Project management or review queue | Makes assigned work and status inspectable. | It records coordination but does not continuously form grounded bets or learn from joined market reality. |

Drover should win only when the founder values the whole loop. If they need a single code change, a
single campaign, or a fixed automation, a focused tool may be better.

## Claim and evidence ledger

Implementation proof is not value proof. Current language must stay inside this boundary.

| Claim | What is true now | Evidence still required |
|---|---|---|
| Drover understands a real product. | Repository binding, scanning, evidence labels, and grounded runtime context exist. | An outside founder recognizes the product, catches few material misunderstandings, and can correct one locally. |
| Drover works while the founder is away. | Heat, activation, budgets, inward work, and away holds exist. | A real venture runs unattended and returns a bounded set of useful staged differences. |
| Drover reduces founder workload. | Whole-firm direction, focus, wall decisions, and return-scene research exist. | The founder spends less time reaching a better real act and can explain the venture state without intervention. |
| Drover keeps the founder in control. | Host-signed, short-lived, single-use founder claims; typed wall mechanics; actor stamping; origin refusal; presence; deploy authorization; and receipts exist. | Observe that outside founders understand and trust the boundary rather than working around it. |
| Drover learns from reality. | Outcomes, joins, provenance, taste, and participant memory exist. | A positive, negative, or zero real outcome changes the next bet in a way the founder recognizes as justified. |
| Drover is better because work stays open-ended. | The host avoids bet kinds, stages, required fields, and routing taxonomies. | Real participants produce coherent divergence without hidden prompt taxonomies or founder-added process machinery. |
| Drover can run a portfolio. | Venture isolation, portfolio wall reads, and portable export/import records exist. | Two real ventures run without bleed, and one imported venture resumes after explicit repository rebinding. |
| Drover improves business outcomes. | No current proof. | Repeated attributable outcomes across founders or ventures, compared with their credible prior way of working. |

## Alpha value hypotheses

The next product proof should test these in order. Measurements belong in research receipts, not as
founder-facing scoreboards.

1. **Specificity:** grounding and accumulated taste produce work that feels native to the product,
   not interchangeable with a generic agent response.
2. **Attention compression:** after unattended work, the founder can identify what changed, what
   needs their hand, and why it matters without exploring the entire firm first.
3. **Review leverage:** verifying and refining a staged act is materially cheaper than producing it
   from scratch.
4. **Authority trust:** the founder is willing to enable always-on work because the wall's boundary is
   clear, exact, and technically credible.
5. **Learning continuity:** a returned outcome changes the next fork while preserving the difference
   between evidence, inference, and lineage.
6. **Portfolio leverage:** adding a second venture increases capability more than it increases
   coordination burden.

The decisive alpha scene is therefore not “an agent completed a task.” It is:

> The founder leaves a grounded venture with a direction, returns to a small number of useful and
> inspectable bets, releases one exact act, receives a real response, and sees that response improve
> the next attempt.

## Consequences for later UX work

Before arranging screens, every important state should be able to answer five questions:

1. What materially changed?
2. Why should the founder believe it?
3. What exact act or judgment needs the founder now?
4. What did reality say, and how certain is the join?
5. What will continue or fork because of it?

Agent internals, provider choice, activity, configuration structure, and the breadth of divergence
should remain available by pull. They should not outrank consequence, evidence, authority, returned
reality, or the next move.
