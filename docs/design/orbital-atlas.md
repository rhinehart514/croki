Founder-picked direction for the whole-journey UI/UX rebuild, chosen from a scored audit (IA 25 · Flow 37 · Craft 48 · Consistency 63) and two diverge rounds. Supersedes the calm-status-board reading of the canvas. The founder's intent, verbatim, is the brief: *"I want to see and visualize my agentic workflows within a GTM standard and be able to explore and iterate on GTM systems and product systems… the orbit should feel deep and complex — but chat makes it easy to use."* Wow moment: *"project X, I want my GTM system to have 3–5 motions of PLG, outbound, etc. and set up these campaigns"* → the whole system materializes on the canvas as a staged, gated proposal.

## The decision

**Dive (altitudes like a map) is the spine; unfold-in-place is the graft.**

- **Venture altitude**: intent fixed at center (serif, the one focal), motions as labeled angular sectors of the orbit, bets on altitude rings where **radius = proximity to a decision**, the wall as the outer amber arc. Every bet carries a **machinery glyph** — a live miniature of its workflow chain (filled = done/running, ringed amber = held at gate, hollow = queued) with real counts. Bets are ALWAYS projected; the canvas may never contradict the header.
- **Unfold graft**: selecting a bet at venture altitude unfolds its workflow chain in place (read without leaving); diving is the commitment.
- **Dive altitude**: one bet's full agentic workflow fills the field — stage nodes with run receipts (never a spinner), teammate presence tags, causality-labeled edges, the amber gate as the hero with a gate inspector showing the exact held artifact, what releasing does, and reversibility. Orientation is sacred: breadcrumb + corner mini-orbit ("you are here") always present.
- **Chat proposes systems**: a plain-words ask materializes motion clusters as ghosted/dashed staged regions — campaigns with workflow chains, assigned teammates, expected signals — every outward node gated amber, accept-all / adjust-per-motion, plain-words receipt ("3 motions · 5 campaigns · 14 stages · 4 gates · nothing runs until you say so"). Reasoning streams beside the assembling canvas. Wire to the real architecture-proposal machinery, not a parallel fake.

## The hand

Existing token family, disciplined: warm stone ground `#e9e6e0`, surface `#f4f2ee`, ink `#23211d`, spruce `#2e6e5e`/`#1e5245` as the single interaction accent, amber `#a9791a` reserved exclusively for wall/gate, proven-green `#5d7350` for returned outcomes. Serif display (Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia) only on venture intent and gate titles; system sans body; mono + `tabular-nums` on every changing figure. Motion shows causality (drift spokes, stages checking off); nothing bounces.

## Vetoes the build must kill first (from the 2026-07-15 audit)

1. Default canvas contradicting its header (empty room vs "7 live bets"); diagnostic strings ("0 architecture elements · placement is presentation only") in founder space.
2. Dual naming: bet vs "What we're testing", outcome vs "Reality returned", teammate vs crew, invented "Board". One name per object, FIRM-SPEC vocabulary.
3. Dead ends: unfinishable Start-venture form; clear wall pointing at nothing; return briefing that can't be finished after "Show the wider firm".
4. Action without consequence: placement renders nothing visible; focal intent occluded by docked chrome.
