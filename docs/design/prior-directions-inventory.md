## Approved direction

Drover's main surface is the venture canvas over one canonical open Product/go-to-market model. One venture
conversation and persistent scoped branches direct and interrogate it. Understand / Design / Execute / Learn
are reversible lenses. Work starts only from founder direction or a founder-invoked outcome contract.
Generated structure is provisional; facts, evidence, and interpretation stay separate. Direct manipulation
is reversible until exact founder-held consequence. Claude/Codex stay visible in provenance, not an AI org
chart.

## Rejected product directions

The following are not current implementation instructions:

- Atlas-only or orbital product identity;
- Now/direction-workspace primacy and optional-canvas framing;
- immersive-only shell and ambient conversation;
- permanent docked inspector or bottom activity console as fixed product regions;
- legacy triptych as a separate product;
- experiment-machine or staged pipeline lifecycle;
- permanent AI crew, configurable AI firm, standing mandates, and ambient work;
- automatic evidence-triggered next cycles;
- workflow builder, executable graph, or default DAG;
- closed Product-loop/System/Motion/Campaign ontology;
- founder-never-drags and engine-owned placement as experience law;
- automatic layout or generated view silently becoming canonical truth.

Useful evidence:

- evidence and consequence relationships visible in space;

`workyard.md`, and `ux-divergence-2026.html`. Orbit metaphysics, fixed roles, and no-drag rules are rejected.

### Contextual precision and founder consequence

Useful evidence:

- exact diff/artifact review;
- purpose-specific consequence verbs;
- a workbench that preserves canvas and conversation context;
- source, provenance, verification, cost, and rollback receipts;
- product-change review before apply and second deploy authorization.

Sources include `connect-capability.md`, `workflow-authoring-contract-audit.md`, `workyard.md`,
`orbital-atlas-brain.md`, and current implementation receipts.

### Product and go-to-market traceability

Useful evidence:

- Product value, audience, positioning, offer, channel, campaign, artifact, response, and learning can be
  spatially related without becoming mandatory host types;
- evidence stays non-causal unless captured proof supports the join;
- Product and market work share artifacts, consequences, and returned evidence;
- open vocabulary and provisional structure prevent a CRM or workflow cage.

Sources include `LIVING-VENTURE-ATLAS-SPEC.md`, `OPEN-CANVAS-SPEC.md`, capability-density explorations, and
frontier research. The approved model is the authority, not any historical role set.

### Runtime and multi-agent work

Useful evidence:

- Claude/Codex provider parity;
- isolated worktrees and branches;
- visible participant scope, verification, checkpoints, cost, and failures;
- independent critique and compare flows;
- stop/redirect only where enforceable;
- no model self-approval.

Historical teammate portraits, rosters, hierarchy, manager agents, standing mandates, and automatic wake-up
remain rejected.
