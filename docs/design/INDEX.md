remain the authorities for intended experience, product physics, and current proof respectively.

## Historical explorations

The remaining Markdown, HTML, and supporting assets in this directory are historical explorations unless an
authority file cites a specific adopted decision. This includes Atlas-first, Now-first, immersive,
workyard, experiment-machine, configurable-firm, workflow-builder, and alternate shell directions. In
particular, `orbital-atlas-*`, `workyard*`, `shell-*`, `REBUILD-HANDOFF.md`, and
`PRODUCTION-UX-PLAN.md` must not be read as current implementation instructions.

## Superseded screenshot and HTML runs

Screenshot folders, standalone HTML prototypes, and generated comparison runs are preserved as visual
evidence of decisions at the time they were made. They do not prove the current tree, and localhost links
inside them are historical receipts. No evidence is moved or deleted until an external archive destination
is explicitly chosen.

When evidence and an authority disagree, record the conflict and follow the authority. Promote a durable
new direction by amending the applicable authority first; do not silently treat this index as adoption.
