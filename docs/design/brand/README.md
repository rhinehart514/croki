# Croki identity

Croki's mark is one fused dog-and-cat face: dog on the left, cat on the right. It is a single character, not
two pets placed beside each other.

## Production assets

- `croki-mark.svg` is the editable master mark.
- `croki-mark-1024.png` is the high-resolution raster export.
- `../../../build/icon.svg` and `../../../build/icon.icns` are the desktop packaging assets.
- `../../../ui/public/favicon.svg` is the product favicon.

## Rules

- Keep the resting mark black and warm white. A single spectrum sweep may cross the face at launch or when
  Croki moves from idle to active; it always settles back to monochrome and never loops, pulses, replaces factual
  progress, or changes the packaged icon and raster assets.
- Preserve the mixed face, floppy dog ear, pointed cat ear, and cat-side whiskers.
- Pair it with the name **Croki** in product and marketing identity.
- Give it strongest presence at installation, launch, onboarding, and marketing moments. Keep it quiet during
  focused work.
- Preserve lowercase internal compatibility identifiers such as package names, storage paths, protocols, and
  application IDs until they receive an intentional migration.
