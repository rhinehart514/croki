## Layer 1 — User goals (the North; every layer below serves these)

The solo founder who vibe-codes wants to:
1. See the whole venture at a glance — product vision down to distribution. *(clarity)*
2. Push new things consistently — one plain intent per campaign, artifact, or product change.
3. Explore and choose GTM + product approaches with confidence.
4. Run many things in parallel without losing control.
5. Decide only what touches the world; trust the rest runs safely.
6. Learn from the market and let it shape what's next.
7. Use their own Claude Code + Codex subscriptions and models — no cage.
8. Return after time away to a clear account, not a mess.

## Layer 2 — Experience architecture (DECIDED — the three bold moves, stacked)

**Drover is one living venture you drive by intent — a generative firm that materializes, runs, and learns.**
Not an app with sections; a place your company lives, that you conduct by talking. Mental model, three words
the founder never has to read: **Say it · Watch it · Release it** — and then *Learn, and say it again.*

Three load-bearing mechanics, one from each ideation, that together are the architecture:

- **A · One object, two views** *(from the console thesis).* The conversation and the canvas are the same
  thing seen two ways. You *act* in the console (plain intent, streamed agent steps, tool-calls, receipts on
  your own subs); the work *materializes and persists* as a node on the canvas. The thread is the node's
  history; the node is the thread's result. Nothing scrolls away — the transcript becomes the venture's
  spatial long-term memory. This resolves the console-vs-canvas question the repo kept circling: neither is
  "the main surface"; they're two projections of one selection model, bidirectionally synced.

- **B · One continuous world, navigated by altitude — no tabs, no modes** *(from the canvas thesis).* Pull
  the camera back: the vision→distribution clarity spine and the calm operating picture (Needs you · Live ·
  Building · Returned). Descend into a way-of-growing: its agentic workflow — agents as nodes with roles,
  experiments and artifacts inline, the wall as a gate node in place. Descend again: a single live artifact.
  Depth = complexity, literal. ⌘K teleports the camera; Escape rises; camera history restores your exact
  mental map. Systems/motions/campaigns/product are *lenses that focus the one world*, never tabs the founder
  administers.

- **C · The signature act is generative, and evidence forks** *(from the ideation thesis).* One plain ask
  blooms a **fan of 2–5 genuinely different approaches**, each a role-cast workflow you compare (a structured
  approach-diff in the inspector), refine, branch ("try another approach"), and commit one to run
  autonomously. And the load-bearing move: **returned evidence forks the next round** — every outcome node
  carries an "explore from here" affordance that seeds the next fan pre-loaded with what the world just said.
  This is what converts Drover from an agent runner into a *compounding learning system*.

The **wall** gates every world-touching act. **Agents** are inhabitants with visible roles (the five kernel
roles drive placement and register, never founder vocabulary), running on the founder's own subs, honestly
metered.

## Layer 3 — Information architecture

**Objects, in founder language** (never `bet/motion/fork/pipeline/stage/outcome/the wall`): the venture · who
it helps · how value happens · ways to grow (approaches) · live pushes · what the agents are doing · what they
made · what the market said · the people on it and what each is doing.

**The spine** (the real architecture kernel, dressed in plain words): product vision → the durable machinery
it's built on → the ways it reaches people → the live pushes → the concrete acts that touch the world.

**Navigation is altitude + focus + teleport — not pages.**
- Altitude (semantic-zoom LOD, hysteresis-quantized): vision picture → operating picture → agentic workflow →
  artifact. Depth-gating hides deeper nodes until you descend.
- Focus (lens): pulls one layer forward, dims the rest to ~0.34 — the whole world stays present.
- ⌘K: jump to any object/action/venture; the camera flies there; sets both console and canvas to it.
- Escape rises one altitude; breadcrumb shows the descent path.

**The three postures map to three verbs, all on one canvas:** *ideate* (the approach fan), *operate* (the calm
home altitude, which must answer "where does everything stand" in one sweep AND host "push a new thing" as a
first-class gesture — the founder's own make-or-break test for killing tabs), *decide* (the wall).

**Placement is engine-owned.** The projection decides what exists (`PLACEHOLDER`); `atlasLayoutEngine` decides
where. Deleting placement regenerates a deterministic atlas, losing only placement.

## Layer 4 — Interaction design

**The loop:** intent → materialize → compare/steer → commit → approve (wall) → return → re-ideate. The founder's
copy names the act; the machine runs `diverge → prepare → founder decision → act → observe → adapt` underneath.

**Core gestures (all plain language or direct manipulation — no forms, no DAG wiring, no setup ceremony):**
- Type intent in the console → a thread opens; inward work starts the same turn; the fan (or the working
  theory) blooms on the canvas, visibly provisional.
- Select two approaches → the inspector shows a structured approach-diff (who each reaches, mechanism, cost,
  which roles, where it's weakest). ⌘K → "compare all."
- Refine ("make B cheaper") edits in place; branch ("try another approach") spawns a dashed sibling. Branching
  is the *only* structural op.
- Commit → the card solidifies (provisional → committed·running), teammates start real work; siblings collapse
  to a quiet "other approaches (N)."
- Double-click to descend; Escape to rise. Select a node → the console scopes to it (scope chip); Escape
  broadens. Bidirectional, always.
- At the wall: read the exact staged act as a diff; Release or Hold; one decision.

## Layer 5 — Feedback & edge cases (enumerated; the states most demos skip)

- **Provisional vs durable:** provisional reads dashed, faintly desaturated, gently drifting; durable is solid.
  You know it's a first draft because it *looks* unsettled — never a label.
- **Agent working vs done:** live step-list with real tool-calls + file/line citations and a pulse on the node;
  done settles to a receipt (runtime · model · cost · config revision, quiet).
- **Held at the wall:** the exact node glows amber *where the work lives*, and the docked wall gains weight;
  typed purpose (release outward · answer · review evidence · end work) — reviewing evidence never masquerades
  as releasing it.
- **Away / offline / stale:** last verified view retained; consequential writes held; presence lease lapses to
  away; away holds all outward effects, inward work continues.
- **Budget exhausted:** the drive pauses and resumes; cost ticks on the founder's own meter; a cost-silent
  runtime is charged a conservative estimate.
- **Zero / negative / conflicting outcomes:** first-class and equal; in the market's own words, never a score;
  unattributed signals stay silent rather than being claimed; a real reply fires the decide-together interrupt.
- **Empty states:** no ventures → the picker; no work → a calm venture with the intent bar and a few suggested
  intents; nothing needs you → the wall is a thin clear edge, not a red badge.
- **Dense scale:** divergence runs wide but quiet *below the fold*; only decisions and fresh forks pull
  attention (amber only). The keyboard outline retains every item even when the canvas quiets it.
- **Provider / send failure:** normalized failure vocabulary, honest retry state; a send that fails renders an
  unavailable state, never a false "clear."
- **Return after away:** `Since you left` — Needs you · The market spoke · Kept moving · Held safely; every item
  returns to a canonical object; unresolved wall work is never cleared by the return cursor.
- **Reduced motion:** removes camera travel and JS animation; keeps the state change.

## Layer 6 — Long-term coherence (invariants that never break)

- **Founder language everywhere** product-owned; internal nouns stay in storage/routes only.
- **The wall is the one world boundary;** the release capability is host-issued at decision time, never
  mintable by a model, browser, API, or MCP actor; deploy keeps its second authorization.
- **No host-composed DAG.** Agents work directly; branching is the only structural op. Approaches are cast by
  the work, not wired by the founder. No status enum — position is derived.
- **One venture = one local file;** one venture store, one architecture document, placement-only canvas state.
  The transcript is the existing per-bet event log — no second authority.
- **Open architecture never a cage.** Agents propose; only the founder makes durable. Configuration can only
  make the firm more restrictive; it never mints outward authority.
- **Runs on the founder's own subs,** visibly. Per-teammate runtime, real auth, real budget, honest cost.
- **Scales across the four axes** (ventures · transfer · founders · functions) with zero new nouns.
- **The five fatal modes are the guardrails;** the three failure-tests below are what real use must disprove.

## The build (all three ideations agreed; this is the sequencing decision)

1. **Flow 1 end-to-end first — the signature slice.** Intent → a materialized firm, with the console↔canvas
   binding real (Move A: thread = node), on real Buffalo data, at 1920×1080, judged by the design-critic.
   STATE.md is explicit this flow *is not built* — it's the whole gap between what ships and this system.
   **Do not build the tab spine.**
2. **The Approach Fan altitude + the outcome-node generator** (Move C). The one net-new projection mode: sibling
   approaches as side-by-side role-cast workflows with a structured diff; outcomes that seed the next fan.
3. **Altitude/LOD polish** (Move B): the continuous dive, hysteresis, depth-gating, camera feel. Everything
   rides on the dive feeling *inevitable*.
4. Rich console (streamed steps, tool-calls, artifacts inline, approval gate), then the wall, then lenses.

Substrate is already there: `@xyflow/react`, the typed projection with its real labeled edges, engine-owned
placement, souls, presence, the wall, the per-bet event log. Most of this system is composition of parts that
exist; the net-new is the Fan altitude and the transcript↔node binding.

## The three failure-tests (honest; only a real venture settles these)

1. **Trust:** will a founder trust work they didn't watch being made? Move A (permanent, spatial,
   inspectable making) is the direct bet. If they won't even when it's fully inspectable, the autonomy premise
   is wrong.
2. **Choice:** do founders want to choose among approaches, or want the machine to just pick and go? If they
   always take the first card, the Fan is overhead and Move C is wrong.
3. **Overwhelm:** does divergence-below-the-fold stay calm at real volume, or flood? Fatal mode #3 is the
   sharpest ongoing risk of a generative, parallel machine.
