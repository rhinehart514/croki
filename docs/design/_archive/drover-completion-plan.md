# Drover completion plan — immediate working theory

**Status:** executable completion sequence, 2026-07-15.
**Authority:** [`FIRM-SPEC.md`](../FIRM-SPEC.md), [`STATE.md`](../STATE.md), and
[`immediate-working-theory.md`](immediate-working-theory.md). This file supersedes the proposal-first
signature and completion order in `orbital-atlas-build.md`; it does not supersede the implemented Atlas
foundation or `orbital-atlas-brain.md` B1–B5.

## Completion claim

One ordinary founder direction must produce, in one coordinating drive:

1. cited product grounding;
2. a source-bearing, visibly provisional whole-venture theory;
3. at least one useful inspectable inward artifact or verified isolated product change; and
4. exact founder-held consequences for anything that sends, publishes, applies, deploys, or creates
   external spend.

The founder does not approve empty architecture first. Theory is supersedable belief; exact work,
receipts, decisions, and returned evidence remain the consequential truth. Historical route and storage
identifiers remain compatibility seams and do not become founder copy.

## Invariants

- Use the existing drive, proposal, architecture, work, evidence, and founder-authority machinery. No
  second engine or theory store.
- Working-theory mode may hold open concepts and descriptive relationships. It cannot mint authority,
  durable operational roles, empty campaigns, or executable workflow state.
- Every visible grouping resolves to founder language, a cited repository fact, exact work, an artifact,
  a relationship, a decision, or returned evidence.
- Preserve semantic subject identity across supersession; record what changed and why.
- Deterministic evidence pressure may render immediately. Semantic reinterpretation requires a visible
  active model turn; never imply invisible reasoning.
- Begin with one coordinator. Parallel continuation is blocked until provider spend and resume state
  have one concurrency-safe writer.
- Provider inference spend remains bounded by the founder-owned daily rail. New external/campaign spend
  remains an exact held consequence.
- Founder-owned copy uses the concrete business meaning. Do not require `bet`, `motion`, `fork`,
  `outcome`, `the wall`, `pipeline`, `stage`, `work item`, or `architecture proposal`.
- Atlas orientation, semantic zoom, outline parity, camera restoration, exact receipts, Dive depth, and
  the still founder consequence boundary remain load-bearing.
- Motion follows real causal state changes only. Pan, zoom, drag, selection cycling, keyboard-frequent
  actions, and held consequences do not animate. Every animation has an instant reduced-motion path.

## WT1 — make the sentence real

Add provider-neutral, repository-bounded search and cited excerpt reads to the existing work loop. Extend
existing proposal state with a restricted `working-theory` mode, stable subject anchors, conversation
references, and supersession identity. Keep `POST /drive` as the coordinating front door.

The host reports the signature turn complete only after grounding, a current theory, and useful
inspectable work are durable. Honest partial progress survives interruption and is presented as stopped,
not completed. Project the newest theory inline with real work, evidence, and held consequences; do not
open `ArchitectureProposalSurface` as the signature result.

**Acceptance:** a clean repository-bound venture receives `Help this venture grow`; the first completed
response shows a cited provisional whole and at least one real artifact/change without architecture
acceptance. Provider-native tool differences cannot change the core grounding contract. Invalid or
ungrounded theory never appears as validated.

## WT2 — make the theory editable

Extend canvas selection and conversation scope to provisional subjects and relationships. A correction
such as `agencies are not the buyer` creates a superseding theory revision, preserves unchanged subject
IDs, re-contextualizes related work, and shows an evidence-backed delta. Give provisional belief its own
runtime context builder; never present it as founder-applied architecture.

Offer durable structure only when it changes future context, reuse, or attribution, through the existing
founder-applied architecture path.

**Acceptance:** a selected-claim correction supersedes rather than overwrites; unchanged work and subject
identity survive; the UI shows the changed claim/relationship and reason; no configuration workflow is
required.

## WT3 — let inward work compound safely

Serialize participant drives or reserve provider spend through durable compare-and-set state before
parallel fan-out. Give participant resume state one writer. Preserve configured runtime, capability,
authority, and daily-spend limits for every involved participant. Automatic continuation may perform
only bounded inward work; restart requires explicit continuation until durable recovery semantics exist.

Return briefs lead with changed understanding, concrete work, returned evidence, and exact founder
decisions. Idle, disconnected, or interrupted providers never render fabricated activity.

**Acceptance:** adversarial overlapping-drive tests cannot overspend the rail or lose resume progress;
process interruption preserves durable partial work; world-touching effects remain held; the return view
describes only real work and evidence.

## WT4 — remove the old opening and machinery register

Delete the signature dependency on whole-system empty assembly and its acceptance overlay. Keep proposal
acceptance for explicit later durable-structure decisions. Make the founder-language guard authoritative
over rendered strings, aria labels, errors, empty states, fixtures, and browser assertions. Retarget the
primary browser journey to sentence → theory → useful work → exact held consequence → evidence/correction.

Rewrite public positioning only after the production slice is coherent and captured. Do not rename
historical implementation identifiers unless a migration buys concrete engineering value.

**Acceptance:** the signature journey contains no empty-architecture approval and no product-owned
machinery nouns; internal routes and records still pass compatibility and authority tests.

## WT5 — causal motion

Use the existing Motion stack and shared tokens. Theory operations materialize only as real validated
records arrive; exact work becomes solid only when durable work exists; workflow receipts settle on real
state changes; returned evidence visibly pressures the relevant relationship. Unfold/fold and Dive/return
preserve object identity and orientation. Held consequences remain still.

**Acceptance:** every transition is transform/opacity, 180–280ms, non-bouncy, and absent for frequent or
keyboard actions. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` renders the final state instantly. The signature moment
is legible without narration and contains no timer-driven fake progress.

## WT6 — accessibility ship gate

Provide deterministic keyboard order across chrome and canvas, then stable node traversal. Enter opens
the selected object; a named nested action enters Dive; Escape folds or returns one altitude. The exact
consequence inspector traps and restores focus. Every control/node has visible focus and an accessible
name. Contrast meets AA on real backgrounds. Color-coded theory, evidence, work, and consequence states
carry a redundant shape, label, or icon.

**Acceptance:** picker → direction → theory → exact work → consequence inspector → return is operable
without a pointer. Automated axe coverage reports zero critical violations on the opening, Atlas, Dive,
correction, and consequence surfaces. Manual focus order, trap/restore, contrast, non-color, and reduced
motion checks are recorded; any dead end blocks completion.

## WT7 — completion and proof

Run:

```sh
npm run test:acceptance
npm run test:atlas:fixtures
git diff --check
```

Capture fresh 1920×1080 evidence from the live app. Recheck 1600×1000, 1440×900, and 1280×800 as
secondary desktop resilience sizes, then recheck the original four vetoes (header/canvas
contradiction, dual naming, dead ends, action without visible consequence) and the retired B1–B5 UI
fallbacks. Give a fresh-context critic the fixed IA and Flow rubrics; both must score at least 80/100.
Anything lower is a fix and a repeated independent review, not a ship.

The final proof is one silent, unedited 1920×1080 clip under 30 seconds:

`ordinary direction → cited theory → useful work → exact consequence held still`

The clip cannot rely on narration, captions, fake phase controls, fixed timers, or accepting a plan as its
climax.

## Two finish lines

**Build complete:** WT1–WT7 pass, independent IA/Flow are both at least 80, and the silent clip proves the
signature interaction.

**Product proven:** a real founder uses the broad direction on a real venture, receives useful work,
corrects the theory naturally, and real returned evidence pressures the picture. Mechanical proof and
independent critique cannot substitute for this result.
