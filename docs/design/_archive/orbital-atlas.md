## The superseded decision

**Historical proposal:** dive was the spine and unfold-in-place was the graft.

- **Venture altitude**: intent fixed at center (serif, the one focal), motions as labeled angular sectors of the orbit, bets on altitude rings where **radius = proximity to a decision**, the wall as the outer amber arc. Every bet carries a **machinery glyph** — a live miniature of its workflow chain (filled = done/running, ringed amber = held at gate, hollow = queued) with real counts. Bets are ALWAYS projected; the canvas may never contradict the header.
- **Unfold graft**: selecting a bet at venture altitude unfolds its workflow chain in place (read without leaving); diving is the commitment.
- **Dive altitude**: one bet's full agentic workflow fills the field — stage nodes with run receipts (never a spinner), teammate presence tags, causality-labeled edges, the amber gate as the hero with a gate inspector showing the exact held artifact, what releasing does, and reversibility. Orientation is sacred: breadcrumb + corner mini-orbit ("you are here") always present.
- **Chat proposes systems**: a plain-words ask materializes motion clusters as ghosted/dashed staged regions — campaigns with workflow chains, assigned teammates, expected signals — every outward node gated amber, accept-all / adjust-per-motion, plain-words receipt ("3 motions · 5 campaigns · 14 stages · 4 gates · nothing runs until you say so"). Reasoning streams beside the assembling canvas. Wire to the real architecture-proposal machinery, not a parallel fake.
