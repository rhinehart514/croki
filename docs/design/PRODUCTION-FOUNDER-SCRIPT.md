# Drover outside-founder proof script

**Authority:** [`../FIRM-SPEC.md`](../FIRM-SPEC.md), [`../STATE.md`](../STATE.md), and the Living
Venture Atlas three-second promise in
[`venture-architecture-adaptation/LIVING-VENTURE-ATLAS-SPEC.md`](venture-architecture-adaptation/LIVING-VENTURE-ATLAS-SPEC.md).  
**Status:** ready to run against the Living Venture Atlas; no outside-founder receipt recorded yet.  
**Supported surface:** desktop at 1440×900 or 1280×800, on the founder's own repository.

## Setup

- Start from a repository-bound venture with an unattended cycle completed and no unintended outward act.
- Do not seed sample architecture or bets, explain the interface, name the return groups, or point at
  the wall, canvas, machinery disclosure, or outline.
- Screen-record only with explicit participant permission. Record timestamps and answers without venture content.
- A researcher may recover a broken environment; any product explanation ends the independent run.

## Script

Open Drover and ask only these questions, in order:

1. What is this venture trying to make true?
2. How does its product create value?
3. Which route to value is active now?
4. What changed while you were away?
5. What needs you now?
6. What did the market say, and why do you trust that join?
7. How would you resume or change the originating work?

Let the founder operate silently for up to three minutes. Do not rephrase a question unless they ask
what a word means. Record the first answer, the first control used, whether exact proof opened, the
decision consequence they expected, whether they recovered to the originating bet, and elapsed time.

## Passing evidence

- At least six of seven answers are correct without a walkthrough.
- The founder identifies the active architecture without opening agent or runtime machinery first.
- The founder opens one exact receipt, distinguishes held work from released work, and predicts the
  consequence of one wall action before taking it.
- They return to the originating bet or exact workpiece with place and draft intact, then choose a
  valid next action.
- Any authority bypass, unintended outward act, cross-venture read, lost record, or unexplained stale
  decision fails the run regardless of comprehension.

Log dated receipts in [`../STATE.md`](../STATE.md). Keep mechanical readiness, live-provider evidence,
outside-founder comprehension, desire, and real-market outcome as separate claims.
