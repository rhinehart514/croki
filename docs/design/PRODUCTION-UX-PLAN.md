## Outcome

Drover reaches production when an outside founder can bind a real product, give the firm one open
direction, leave, return to a bounded set of decisions, inspect the proof behind a surprising claim,
act with the exact consequence understood, and receive a real-world outcome without a walkthrough or
an unintended outward act.

Drover reaches the frontier when that loop feels like returning to a capable firm already in motion,
not supervising agents or operating a workflow builder. The return is compressed to conviction; the
conversation explains; the canvas proves; the wall holds authority; the founder changes the work in
place.

The production wedge is one venture. Portfolio return, cross-machine transfer, and two-venture
operation follow only after the single-venture loop is proved. Deterministic tests can prove mechanics;
they cannot declare either horizon achieved.

## Non-negotiable experience contract

These rules translate `FIRM-SPEC.md`, root `DESIGN.md`, and the live product into build constraints.

1. **The return outranks the prompt.** In three seconds the founder can tell what changed, what needs
   them, and what the world said.
2. **The wall is the return surface.** Do not create a results inbox, notifications product, or second
   attention authority.
3. **The firm speaks; the canvas proves.** A consequential sentence opens the durable records that
   support it. A summary with no receipt path is not allowed.
4. **Selection preserves place.** Focus changes altitude and context, not route or camera without an
   explicit restoration target.
5. **A bet is the atomic work focus.** Teammate focus is attribution across bets, not a fabricated
   causal thread.
6. **Truth has visible grammar.** Exact receipt, evidence-supported join, teammate inference, and
   unattributed signal remain distinguishable without relying on color.
7. **Work is watchable and leaveable.** Show real events and durable artifacts; never manufacture a
   checklist, progress percentage, or looping productivity theater.
8. **Stop work is not end bet.** Canceling an active runtime preserves the bet and its staged evidence.
   Only the founder ends the line through the wall.
9. **Configuration is use, not administration.** One open first direction enters the real shell;
   participant, runtime, heat, spend, and capability choices appear only when consequential.
10. **No machinery creep.** Presentation groups and focus arrangements project existing records.
    They do not introduce task, workflow, stage, run, notification, or result-inbox authorities.
11. **Outcome means returned reality.** Do not use outcome for a desired future. Preserve historical
    implementation identifiers such as `GoalComposer`, `goal`, `crew`, and `gtm-ide`; fix only
    founder-facing language.
12. **Desktop only.** Judge 1440x900 and 1280x800. Do not spend this plan on phone or tablet behavior.

## Research decisions

Mobbin was used as shipped evidence, not as a component catalog. Every reference below was inspected
as an image or full flow before extraction.

### First use

- [Cursor's repository-first agent entry](https://mobbin.com/screens/b0eb16b6-7038-4d01-a59b-3a1e8a602dd4)
  keeps the execution boundary beside the first prompt. Transfer repository + direction adjacency;
  reject task-manager chrome.
- [Typeform's create-first onboarding](https://mobbin.com/flows/1856adf8-41de-4886-882b-18ed02a2d763)
  postpones consequential choices until the user is inside a real project. Transfer the immediate
  return to the normal workspace; reject templates as Drover's first object.
- [FLORA's in-canvas teaching](https://mobbin.com/flows/88400140-0afc-49eb-8efb-0efc11688207)
  preserves spatial context. Transfer contextual explanations; reject the seven-step tour and empty
  canvas as prerequisites.
- [Synthesia's inspectable pre-generation outline](https://mobbin.com/flows/aee659a6-8d96-4d36-9ed9-68410b1514a6)
  proves early output should be editable. Transfer inspectability; reject forced topic/audience/tone
  fields that would hardcode the substance of a bet.

### Delegate, watch, and review

- [Manus's task-and-artifact split](https://mobbin.com/flows/153b7f2d-db6c-4ee0-a703-e322d3e453dc)
  keeps progress, steering, and the artifact together. Transfer the adjacent artifact and live
  correction; reject agent-authored steps as proof.
- [Relevance AI's correction flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/bfb13164-7400-4c9e-b50a-430462ef9f63)
  preserves the original direction, updates, result, and correction in one line of work. Transfer
  inspectable revision and rerun; reject its generic task/status model.
- [WRITER's active stop and deliverable split](https://mobbin.com/flows/05b3c653-52ee-4323-8170-2cb7e314c0da)
  proves a visible stop can coexist with ongoing work and adjacent output. Transfer stop-current-work;
  reject generic quality feedback as an authority decision.
- [Reddit's queue-plus-detail moderation](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef1ae11f-e221-43cf-84d4-a723ad7ae8c0)
  preserves queue position while an item is inspected. Transfer context retention and item-specific
  actions; reject a universal approval verb.

### Canvas and recovery

- [WRITER's stable blueprint canvas](https://mobbin.com/screens/7bb09b9c-de27-4a19-8183-36df6bb26afc)
  preserves the graph while inspection changes. Transfer stable place; reject a permanent third pane
  at Drover's supported desktop widths.
- [Zoom's anchored object editing](https://mobbin.com/flows/864a60cd-9dd7-4b70-aa60-a35a3b0ac9b0)
  keeps selection and local tools attached through changes. Transfer anchored, reversible local
  actions and undo; reject authoring-tool chrome that competes with the firm.
- [Fibery's version preview](https://mobbin.com/screens/714aa790-1b39-4ef7-ab48-9402ebeb7aea)
  previews an attributable historical state before restore. Transfer preview-before-restore and a new
  revision receipt; never rewrite firm history.

### Return

- [Linear's inbox split](https://mobbin.com/flows/0ebbf5cb-6487-4466-9b42-58704b3773a9)
  keeps a queue visible while canonical source context opens beside it. Transfer retained position and
  source context; do not create a Drover inbox noun.
- [Asana's requires-your-action summary](https://mobbin.com/screens/ce717ee0-8445-49d2-9dda-227a396cef9a)
  leads an async return with the underlying item that needs action. Transfer consequence-first
  compression; reject an uncited summary as authority.
- [Klarna's delivery detail](https://mobbin.com/flows/8d4d88a6-34fb-49be-be19-b8ab312010d3)
  is the unrelated-domain import: plain current truth first, explicit source next, full history on
  request. Transfer that disclosure order; reject the milestone bar because bets have no lifecycle.

## Delivery sequence

Each batch ends in observable product evidence. Do not start the next founder-visible batch while the
prior batch's blocking gate is red. Foundation and test-fixture work may run alongside a batch when it
does not change the product direction.

### Batch 0 — freeze the proof harness

**Intent:** make production claims falsifiable before changing the surface.

- Add one deterministic `overnight venture` fixture containing 12 inward events, four staged
  artifacts, three typed wall items, one joined reply, one unattributed signal, two safely held outward
  acts, and one failed/partial teammate turn.
- Add one dense fixture with 12 teammates, 120 bets, 30 outcomes, 20 wall items, long copy, and mixed
  fork depth. Use it only in tests and review builds; production first use never gets sample nodes.
- Record the baseline audit screenshots at 1440x900 and 1280x800, normal and reduced motion.
- Create an outside-founder script that asks only: what changed, what needs you, what did the market
  say, why do you trust it, and how do you resume the bet?
- Instrument locally and privately: time to first grounded value, return-to-first-decision, proof
  opens, corrections, releases/rejections, abandoned wall items, and stale/offline incidents. Never
  capture venture content outside its local authority.

**Done:** the fixtures reproduce the current failures and the research script can be run without a
product walkthrough.

### Batch 1 — honest substrate

**Intent:** the product must be safe and truthful before it becomes more autonomous.

- Close the unstamped-loopback authority gap with one host-issued, volatile founder capability. Apply
  it to every founder write; retain actor stamps, presence lapse, cross-origin refusal, deploy's second
  authorization, and cross-venture 404 behavior.
- Add one shell-owned connection/freshness model over conversation, lens, wall, heat, and presence.
  Preserve the last good view with `Updated <time>`; show reconnect/backoff; disable outward and
  configuration writes while stale. A technical connection state is infrastructure, not a bet status.
- Make startup, server restart, provider disconnect, poll failure, partial drive failure, and write
  conflict explicit and recoverable. Never erase staged work to recover UI state.
- Establish an abort seam per active provider drive before showing `Stop current work`. If a runtime
  cannot honor it, do not show a lying control.

**Primary seams:** `brain/src/routes/founder-authority.mjs`, `brain/src/firm/routes.mjs`,
`brain/src/firm/configuration-routes.mjs`, `brain/src/firm/heat-routes.mjs`,
`brain/src/firm/work-routes.mjs`, `ui/src/FirmApp.tsx`, `ui/src/api.ts`, and a feature-local connection
state component/hook.

**Done:** forged local writes fail; stale/offline is visible within one failed request; no outward,
configuration, heat, or product-change action can execute stale; recovery preserves selection, draft,
and last good content.

### Batch 2 — first use reaches real value

**Intent:** enter the production shell after the minimum binding choice and prove value on the
founder's own product.

- Keep venture name + repository binding. Remove `goal` and desired `outcome` from founder copy.
- After creation, focus one open direction in the normal composer: `What should this venture
  accomplish first?` The repository name and `Nothing leaves without your hand` remain visible.
- The first useful response is a cited reading of the bound repository plus teammate-judged divergent
  bets. Do not show setup confirmation, fake sample bets, role questions, or a fixed number of forks.
- Add at most three contextual explanations: first work, first bet focus, first wall item. Each explains
  the immediate consequence, is dismissible, and never blocks work.
- Defer runtime, participant composition, heat, spend, capability, and relationship choices until
  relevant. Keep natural-language and direct-manipulation configuration in the same shell.

**Primary seams:** `ui/src/components/firm/VenturePicker.tsx`, `FirmLensEmpty.tsx`,
`GoalComposer.tsx`, `ConversationFeed.tsx`, `EventFeed.tsx`, and repository truth in
`brain/src/firm/truth.mjs` / `work-loop.mjs`. Keep historical filenames and API identifiers.

**Done:** an outside founder binds a repository and reaches one cited, product-specific piece of work
without configuring the firm, reading a tour, or seeing seeded content.

### Batch 3 — return briefing over the wall

**Intent:** make unattended work compress to founder conviction without creating a second inbox.

- When no object is selected and new durable records exist beyond the founder's last reviewed cursor,
  render a short `Since you left` account over the existing conversation/wall surface. Presence still
  determines safety; the return cursor determines only what the presentation calls new.
- Keep a presentation-only per-venture return cursor for the last reviewed durable records. Losing the
  cursor may replay history but can never hide or resolve a wall item. It is not a notification store,
  work status, decision receipt, or authority; presence remains the volatile safety lease.
- Group presentation, not storage, into: **Needs you**, **The market spoke**, **Kept moving**, and
  **Held safely**. Unresolved wall items can never be cleared by marking passive activity seen.
- Each sentence/card starts with plain current truth, then why it matters now, the exact valid action,
  teammate/bet/time, and a compact provenance strip. `View receipt`, `Open on canvas`, and `Continue
  with teammate` lead to canonical records.
- Add `Show the wider firm` and an explicit omission statement. The account may prioritize but may not
  imply completeness.
- Resolving an item leaves its durable receipt in place and advances predictably. Return-to-wall keeps
  queue scroll, canvas camera, selection, and composer draft.
- Derive the briefing from bets, outcomes, decisions, wall items, conversation, events, presence, and
  firm revision. Do not add a summary store, notification object, urgency score, or lifecycle enum.

**Primary seams:** a feature-local return projection in `brain/src/firm/` or UI selector over an
extended lens payload; `ConversationFeed`, `TeammateRail`, `FirmWallReview`, and `FirmLens`. Domain code
must not import UI; any projection remains read-only over venture records.

**Done:** with the overnight fixture, a founder answers all five research questions in three minutes,
never confuses passive activity with a decision, reaches exact provenance in one disclosure, and
understands that nothing outward happened while away.

### Batch 4 — evidence-backed bet focus

**Intent:** selecting a bet should reveal the whole decision context without leaving or losing place.

- Preserve camera and visible neighborhood on selection. Replace single-node `fitView` with a temporary
  focus scene that includes the bet, fork parent/children, necessary participants, staged artifact or
  diff, evidence, wall dependency, joined outcome, and truth labels.
- Far view shows presence, consequential bets, wall pressure, new returns, and quiet collapsed
  divergence. Middle view shows a bet family and what changed. Near view shows evidence, staged work,
  configuration provenance, decisions, market voice, and local refinement.
- Render semantic node variants; never shrink full paragraphs into texture. The outline remains the
  deterministic keyboard and scale fallback.
- Use a compact anchored peek or temporary canvas detail layer, not a permanent third inspector.
- Add camera history and restoration targets: `fit firm`, `back to focus`, previous/next camera, Escape,
  return to wall, and return to conversation. Manual placement alone persists; generated focus layouts
  do not.
- Refactor scoped conversation so only relevant founder turns, teammate work, handoffs, and receipts
  appear. A firm configuration receipt appears only when it formed or changed the selected bet; raw
  chronology remains secondary.

**Primary seams:** split `FirmLens.tsx` before adding responsibility; extend `firmLensGraph.ts`,
`BetNode.tsx`, `lensLayout.ts`, `FirmLensOutline.tsx`, and the lens read projection. Add feature-local
scene, altitude, and camera-history modules. Keep every production UI component under 300 lines.

**Done:** the dense fixture passes the full restoration journey at both desktop sizes: select a bet,
inspect evidence, open the market return, continue in conversation, open the wall, and back out to the
same selected bet in the same visible neighborhood.

### Batch 5 — watchable work and local correction

**Intent:** trust should come from durable progress and cheap correction, not surveillance.

- Replace the anonymous pulse with actual events emitted by the existing bounded bet log: repository
  grounding, taste consultation, fork, durable artifact, wall hold, failure, and teammate speech.
- Show elapsed time, first durable artifact as it lands, runtime/model/configuration revision, and real
  or conservative cost as quiet receipts. Do not expose chain of thought, raw tool names, token traces,
  or invented percentages.
- Keep the scoped composer available. A correction attaches to the selected bet and preserves the
  prior artifact/revision for comparison.
- Offer `Stop current work` only through the proven abort seam. It preserves the bet and staged records.
  `End bet` remains a founder-only wall decision.
- Partial and failed work stays inspectable with a clear recovery action: retry current move, redirect,
  continue with another configured teammate, or stop. Do not silently restart.

**Primary seams:** `brain/src/firm/work-loop.mjs`, `work-loop-tools.mjs`, runtime adapters,
`work-routes.mjs`, `ui/src/components/firm/event-feed-model.ts`, `ConversationFeed.tsx`, and
`GoalComposer.tsx`.

**Done:** a founder can leave during a real provider turn, return, identify the last durable step,
inspect partial work, correct or stop it, and distinguish stopping execution from ending the bet.

### Batch 6 — sacred wall and outcome loop

**Intent:** make the authority boundary the clearest and calmest part of the product.

- Pending wall focus uses retained queue + exact detail inside the current workbench. The item order is:
  why attention is needed; exact act; recipient/destination; proof; consequence; revision crossing the
  boundary; purpose-valid actions.
- Preserve the current decision matrix. Release/reject, answer/dismiss, acknowledge, kill/keep, and
  deploy's second authorization remain distinct. Revision or fork actions route through the scoped
  composer; they do not masquerade as wall decisions.
- Preview the exact outward artifact or product diff beside the decision. Keep audit fields one
  disclosure deeper, never on another route.
- The clear wall remains a compact band; opening it must not cover the canvas, replace the composer,
  or claim urgency.
- Outcome review leads with the market's words. Show whether the join is provider-backed,
  evidence-supported, inferred, or unattributed. No sentiment colors, score, or aggregate market
  dashboard.
- After a founder decision, render the durable receipt and make the effect on the bet/canvas visible.

## Risks and kill conditions

- **The return summary manufactures certainty.** Kill any sentence that cannot open the durable records
  supporting it. If founders inspect the whole graph before trusting the account, the compression
  failed.
- **The wall becomes a queue product.** Kill new inbox routes, unread authorities, clear-all semantics,
  generic approvals, and notification storage. Presentation groups must disappear without data loss.
- **Canvas focus hides necessary context.** Preserve an omission boundary, wider-firm recovery, shared
  evidence, conflicting effects, and wall dependencies. A clean picture is not worth false coherence.
- **Semantic zoom turns into multiple object models.** Variants may change representation, never domain
  meaning or persistence.
- **Watchable work becomes chain-of-thought theater.** Show only real host-observable events, artifacts,
  costs, and receipts. If a runtime cannot expose a step honestly, show less.
- **Stop promises what a provider cannot do.** Hide the control until abort is enforceable and tested.
- **Configuration review becomes a settings application.** Lead with founder consequence and keep exact
  schema as receipt. Do not split the single firm document into administrative pages.
- **The design system pass becomes speculative.** Consolidate existing duplication and promote earned
  tokens only. Do not build components for future surfaces.
- **Portfolio arrives before the wedge proves.** Hold Batch 9 until Batch 8. Multi-venture chrome cannot
  rescue an untrusted single-venture return.
- **Motion becomes the brand instead of causality.** On this high-frequency return surface, frontier
  means calmer, faster, and clearer. Any addition that survives only because it looks premium is
  gilding and should be removed.
