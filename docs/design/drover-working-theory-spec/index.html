A self-contained product + technical brief · 15 July 2026

The venture starts working while you can still see the whole thing.

Drover is a local-first desktop workbench for a solo founder running one or more products. This document explains the next move: one ordinary sentence draws an editable view of how the business could work, starts useful work, and changes its mind in public as reality returns.

Start here · the 90-second version

Drover already has the machinery. We are changing when the founder feels its value.

This is a proposal for what to build next, not a description of everything already shipped. Today Drover can read a product repository, coordinate AI teammates, prepare research and code changes, hold risky actions for the founder, and learn from real replies. But it introduces that power through internal concepts and an architecture-approval step. The proposed experience starts with useful work and makes the system understandable as it forms.

Built today

Exists in the current codebase and has mechanical coverage.

Build next

The product and technical direction specified in this document.

Still unproven

Requires real founder use or a real market result, not another test.

Who it is for

A solo founder operating several products who wants the leverage of an expert team without becoming a manager of AI agents.

What Drover is today

A desktop application bound to the founder’s real product repository. It gives restricted AI teammates a shared conversation, a deep canvas, isolated code workspaces, connected Gmail, durable evidence, and a founder-controlled release boundary.

The current problem

The machinery is capable, but the opening asks the founder to understand Drover’s internal nouns and approve an empty system shape before enough useful work is visible.

The proposed change

The founder says what should change in normal language. Drover immediately shows its best current interpretation of the whole venture and begins the safe work that can test or improve that interpretation.

What does not change

Every send, publish, product apply, deploy, or spend remains an exact founder decision. Existing work identity, evidence lineage, repository isolation, budgets, and security checks remain authoritative.

What success feels like

The founder does not watch an agent finish a task. They watch the venture become more legible and more capable, then intervene only where their judgment or authority is actually required.

Plain definitions

Four ideas used throughout

Venture
One product or business inside the founder’s portfolio, bound to its real repository and history.
Working theory
Drover’s editable current explanation of who may care, how value happens, how to reach them, and what is worth trying. It is visibly provisional.
Inward work
Reading, analysis, research, drafting, coordination, and isolated code changes that do not touch customers or production.
World-touching consequence
A send, publish, deploy, product apply, or spend. Drover prepares it; the founder decides it.
One concrete storyA founder opens a new product and says: “Help this venture grow.”
01 · ReadDrover grounds itself

It reads the actual product, prior founder decisions, current work, connected reach, and returned evidence.

02 · ExplainA whole view appears

The canvas shows who may benefit, how value could happen, and several plausible ways to reach people—clearly labeled as Drover’s current read.

03 · WorkUseful work begins

Research, product analysis, drafts, or an isolated code change appear under concrete names, with owners and receipts.

04 · HoldThe exact consequence stops

When something is ready to send, apply, deploy, or spend, the founder sees precisely what will happen and why.

05 · LearnReality pressures the view

A reply, adoption result, rejection, or founder correction attaches to the relevant claim. The next active inward turn may strengthen, weaken, split, or replace it.

01

Define the category by the change it causes.

“ADE” is useful internally, but it is not the product copy. The category definition should tell us what Drover must do, what it must refuse to become, and why the canvas exists.

Internal category definition Venture development environment

A place where a founder develops the venture itself—not only its code, campaigns, tasks, or agent configuration.

Founder-facing promise

Say what should change. Drover makes the venture legible, starts the work it can safely do, and brings back the exact decisions only you can make.

Not a task manager

The primary object is the founder’s changing understanding of the venture, backed by real work—not a queue of assigned containers.

Not an agent org chart

Teammates remain inspectable machinery. They do not become the opening metaphor or make the founder configure a company before getting value.

Not a GTM diagram

The canvas is executable evidence: every visible grouping opens into current work, an artifact, a repository fact, a founder statement, or returned reality.

Not autonomous theater

Drover may read, reason, prepare, and change isolated code. It cannot send, publish, deploy, or spend without the founder at the exact consequence.

Not a new glossary

The interface uses business meaning and the founder’s words. Internal precision stays in code, durable receipts, and developer diagnostics.

03

These invariants keep the magic from turning into overhang.

They are design and architecture tests. A feature that violates one is not “more powerful”; it is a second product hiding inside Drover.

01

Theory now, structure later.

Drover can show a complete interpretation immediately. Only patterns that improve reuse, context, or attribution need to become durable structure.

02

No empty architecture.

Every visible grouping must point to founder language, repository truth, concrete work, an artifact, returned evidence, or a real relationship.

03

Ordinary language is the control surface.

The founder revises a claim or relationship by saying what is wrong. They do not translate their judgment into a Drover taxonomy.

04

One loop, many views.

Conversation, canvas, focused work, return briefing, and decision review project the same durable execution truth. None gets its own business store.

05

Autonomy grows inward.

Reading, analysis, drafting, coordination, and isolated code changes can compound. World-touching consequences remain exact founder acts.

06

Evidence pressures the picture.

Reality attaches to the claim it bears on immediately. Semantic reinterpretation happens only during visible model work and preserves the reason for the change.

07

Depth belongs on the canvas.

Chat stays the easy handle. The canvas keeps the system visible, traceable, editable, and satisfyingly complex at desktop scale.

08

Models judge; hosts enforce.

The model decides which paths are promising and when structure may help. The host enforces identity, references, budgets, authority, and isolation.

09

No fixed completeness quota.

No required number of paths, campaigns, teammates, or artifacts. The response is complete when the current venture is coherent and usefully active.

04

Change the sequence, not the ambition.

The current code contains the right powers in the wrong opening order. The migration removes the approval ceremony before value and preserves every consequential rail beneath it.

Minimal proposal evolution

A theory is not another domain model.

Add a mode and explicit anchors to the existing proposal record, but give the mode narrower semantics. A working theory may contain open concepts and plain-language relationships; it must not mint provisional campaigns or empty operational containers. Real work is joined into the view from the existing execution records. Only structural-change mode can be accepted into durable architecture.

Host/model boundary

The host validates venture scope, stable identity, source existence, allowed theory operations, authority, budget, and compare-and-set revision. It derives “active,” “prepared,” and “needs you” only from real records. It does not decide how many paths are enough, whether an audience is promising, or when two ideas mean the same thing. Those remain model judgments, made inspectable through sources.

07

The direction survives. The naive implementation does not.

The stack can produce the signature moment without a new engine, but only if Drover treats provisional understanding, real execution, and returned evidence as different kinds of truth. This is the engineering pressure test: where the idea bends, what must be guaranteed by code, and what remains intentionally unpromised.

VerdictTechnically viable

The current drive, proposal, projection, artifact, product-change, market-lineage, polling, and founder-authority systems cover most of the path.

Best leverageJoin the stack; do not duplicate it

A flexible theory view over existing truth is more powerful than another hierarchy of stored containers.

Largest riskArtifact theater with convincing motion

A generated map and assigned tasks can look intelligent while producing no source-grounded work or consequence.

Reuse proposal machinery, not proposal ontology.

The existing proposal record gives us versioning, compare-and-set writes, provenance, staleness, and ghost projection. Its rigid system → motion → campaign roles are the wrong substrate for provisional thought. Working-theory mode should allow open concepts and relationships only. Operational paths appear because real work joins the scene—not because the model predeclared empty campaign containers.

Guarantee completion in the host, not in prompt wording.

A model can always stop early. The drive may call the right tools and still return a plan instead of useful work. The host must validate the durable effects before reporting success: product truth was inspected, a source-bearing theory exists, and at least one inspectable artifact, analysis, or isolated product change exists. Otherwise the result is honestly partial and resumable.

Evidence creates pressure before it creates interpretation.

An attributable reply can immediately attach to the exact work that caused it and mark a connected claim as challenged or supported. It cannot semantically rewrite the theory unless a model is actually running. With autonomous inward work off, Drover should say “new evidence may change this view,” then revise on the next founder or budgeted inward turn.

Release-kill conditions
  1. A clean venture cannot produce a source-grounded theory and one credible artifact in one drive without manual prompt surgery.
  2. The same broad direction produces generic plans across materially different repositories.
  3. A founder cannot distinguish inferred, grounded, active, and waiting-for-me states at a glance.
  4. The theory requires a second store or a parallel execution schema to function.
  5. Five real corrections leave duplicated subjects, broken selection, or manual diagram grooming.
  6. The produced work has no plausible route to a founder-held world consequence and returned evidence.
