# Immediate Working Theory — selected direction

**Status:** current direction for broad founder asks, selected 2026-07-15.
**Authority:** refines the signature interaction and language rules in
[`orbital-atlas.md`](orbital-atlas.md). The Atlas depth, continuous canvas, and evidence-backed dive
remain. The proposal-first opening and founder-facing object vocabulary do not.

## Product claim

One ordinary sentence gives the founder an immediate, editable view of how the venture could work
and begins the first useful inward actions. The view changes as real work and evidence return.

This combines the two founder picks:

- **Work forms the system:** lasting structure is compressed from real attempts, not designed as an
  empty blueprint first.
- **Show a working theory immediately:** Drover does not withhold the whole-system magic until enough
  evidence has accumulated.

The immediate view is a theory, not a fact. The work underneath it is real.

## Signature interaction

The founder says: **Help this venture grow.**

In the same turn Drover:

1. reads the selected venture, product truth, prior decisions, configuration, and current work;
2. composes a whole-venture interpretation directly on the canvas—who may benefit, how value
   happens, several concrete ways to reach people, and campaigns worth trying;
3. begins safe inward work that can make the interpretation more useful: research, drafts, product
   analysis, code changes in isolation, audience work, or campaign preparation;
4. anchors every visible grouping to concrete work, an artifact, captured evidence, or a real
   relationship;
5. marks inferred structure as provisional and lets the founder revise it in ordinary language; and
6. holds each send, publish, deploy, or spend at its exact consequence for a founder decision.

The founder never approves an empty architecture before seeing value. A structure decision may
still be offered later when making a recurring pattern durable would improve reuse, context, or
attribution.

## Information hierarchy

1. **What Drover thinks is happening** — the editable whole-venture theory.
2. **What is actually being done** — concrete product and market work, with owners and receipts.
3. **Why the theory says that** — founder statements, repository evidence, returned evidence, and
   clearly labeled inference.
4. **What needs the founder** — exact world-touching consequences, never a generic approval queue.
5. **What changed in the theory** — strengthened, weakened, split, or newly inferred relationships.

No layer is a grid of object types. Semantic zoom changes the amount of detail, not the founder's
vocabulary.

## Language contract

Product-owned copy names the actual business meaning. Internal precision remains in code and
receipts.

| Internal reasoning | Founder sees |
| --- | --- |
| bet | “Rewrite the first-run flow,” “Contact these 20 buyers,” or the founder's own phrase |
| motion | “Help new users succeed on their own” or “Reach agencies with this problem” |
| fork | “Try another approach,” “Make a version for agencies,” or “Scale this” |
| outcome | “3 teams activated,” “No one replied,” or the exact returned evidence |
| wall | “Ready to send,” “Ready to deploy,” “Needs your answer,” or the exact decision |
| stage / status | the real artifact, current action, or reason work is waiting |

Do not replace these terms with a new Drover glossary. The absence of a universal founder-facing
noun for “unit of work” is intentional.

## Canvas behavior

- The first theory assembles in place; it does not appear inside a giant document overlay.
- Tentative structure is visually distinct from founder-stated or evidence-supported structure.
- Every group opens into the concrete work and evidence that justify its presence.
- Empty campaigns, routes, or work territories are omitted. Completeness is conceptual, not a quota
  of containers.
- The founder can rewrite a label or relationship in place and see affected work re-contextualize.
- Continued work may split, merge, strengthen, or weaken the theory. The host does not hardcode a
  repetition threshold; the model proposes, evidence is attached, and the founder makes durable
  architecture explicit.
- Evidence-backed focus remains the depth interaction: selecting any claim expands its concrete
  work, receipts, founder decisions, and returned evidence without leaving the canvas.

## Codebase leverage

Use the existing stack as one flow rather than adding another object system:

- `GoalComposer` already carries venture and selection scope into `driveTeammate`.
- Product truth, taste, configuration, participant involvement, branching, artifact preparation,
  founder questions, and outward classification already exist as work-loop tools.
- Architecture proposals are staged, versioned, and attributable; keep them for durable structural
  changes, not as the gate before useful work.
- The current proposal ghost projection can become the provisional-theory projection. The giant
  `ArchitectureProposalSurface` should not own the signature moment.
- Existing exact work, isolated product worktrees, active-drive receipts, founder-decision records,
  Gmail returns, and outcomes provide the evidence anchors the theory needs.
- Selection-scoped conversation and Atlas focus already provide semantic zoom for seeing. The new
  flow makes the same scope scale the consequence of doing.

Historical identifiers such as `bet`, `fork`, and `outcome` remain in routes, storage, and tests
until an intentional migration. New product copy must not expose them.

## Implementation boundary

Do not add a second orchestration engine, architecture store, task taxonomy, fixed number of routes,
or host-owned emergence score. The thinnest coherent implementation is:

1. broaden a founder direction into a provisional architecture projection and concrete inward work;
2. stream only real durable changes into the existing conversation and canvas;
3. let the same work loop prepare exact artifacts;
4. route world-touching effects through existing founder authority; and
5. let evidence-backed structural changes use the existing proposal and revision machinery.

## Proof

The direction is proved when a clean venture can receive **Help this venture grow** and, without an
architecture acceptance step:

- show a legible whole-venture theory within the first response;
- create at least one useful inward artifact or verified product change;
- show no product-owned `bet`, `motion`, `fork`, `outcome`, `the wall`, `pipeline`, `stage`, or
  `work item` copy;
- trace every visible grouping to concrete work, evidence, or a real relationship;
- keep all sends, publishes, deploys, and spend held for the founder; and
- let the founder revise the theory in plain language without opening a configuration workflow.

The silent-clip test remains: sentence → venture appears → useful work becomes visible → exact
founder decisions are held. The clip fails if its climax is accepting a plan rather than seeing the
venture begin to work.
