Draft: the minimum buildable version

The shortest path to one real, checkable buyer signal — deliberately ahead of any scraping, storage, or scheduling infrastructure.

  • 1Pick one narrow subreddit where buyers describe the problem in their own words, and read the last week by hand to confirm signals exist.
  • 2Write the signal test as a single prompt — post in, "is this a buyer signal, and why" out — before writing any code.
  • 3Run it over ~50 real posts and score precision against the hand read. If it can't beat a keyword filter, the wedge isn't here.
  • 4Only then add fetching, dedup, and scheduling — the infrastructure that makes a proven signal continuous.
