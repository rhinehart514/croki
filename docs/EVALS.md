# Drover evals

**Status:** current acceptance contract, revised 2026-07-15.

[FIRM-SPEC.md](FIRM-SPEC.md) defines the product. [STATE.md](STATE.md) is the only authority for
what the current tree proves. These evals define the evidence required to advance that state; they
do not turn an unrun criterion into a completion claim. Superseded repository-change and GTM-engine
evals are preserved in [history/LEGACY-EVALS.md](history/LEGACY-EVALS.md).

## 1. One product language

- Founder surfaces use Drover, venture, teammate, bet, outcome, fork, and the wall.
- **Outcome** means a positive, negative, or zero response from the world. Founder direction,
  configuration intent, and desired change never masquerade as an outcome.
- Bets have no founder-facing kind, stage, workflow, or stored lifecycle status. Their position is
  derived as live, at the wall, or ended by the founder.
- Pipeline appears only in historical compatibility or founder-authored content. Historical
  implementation identifiers such as `gtm-ide` and `channel` remain intact.

## 2. Venture truth and isolation

- A founder can create or reopen a venture bound to one real product repository.
- Product claims cite repository evidence or identify inference.
- Cross-venture reads, writes, wall decisions, and returned outcomes fail closed.
- Durable venture records remain local, readable, exportable, and honest about machine-local paths.

## 3. Living Venture Atlas

- The default canvas shows venture intent, product value, reusable systems, active motions and
  campaigns, wall pressure, and returned reality before teammate machinery.
- Open concepts, connections, and groups can remain descriptive. Only founder-applied product-loop,
  system, motion, and campaign roles change execution context, attribution, or evidence.
- Architecture selection changes the same durable conversation and bounded runtime brief. A later
  architecture revision marks active drives stale rather than silently rewriting their context.
- Campaign activation creates or forks its governing bet through the existing bet store. The Atlas
  projects exact work, wall acts, and outcomes by durable lineage and never creates parallel copies.
- Founder edits use compare-and-set revisions and restorable receipts. Teammates and MCP clients may
  read qualified context and propose changes, but cannot apply, approve, or activate them.
- Pan, zoom, authored placement, camera history, semantic focus, outline parity, dense omission, and
  empty/offline states remain usable at every supported desktop viewport and browser zoom. Deleting
  placement loses no semantic architecture or consequential record.

## 4. Directing the firm

- One persistent conversation sits beside the live canvas.
- The composer is the action spine. With nothing selected it addresses the venture; selecting an
  architecture element, teammate, bet, or exact workpiece adds one exact scope chip and narrows the
  same conversation without opening a second workflow surface or implying access to another venture.
- Every command names its target and exact next consequence. Teammate turns expose the structured
  act, what changed, and a receipt; runtime/model/cost remain quiet provenance.
- Claude Code and Codex report their real runtime, model, authentication mode, and configuration
  revision. Missing runtime access produces a recoverable, truthful state.
- Runtime/model/configuration machinery remains available by pull and never displaces the Atlas as
  the opening product surface.

## 5. Bets, forks, and product work

- A teammate can answer, fork genuinely different bets, consult taste, stage artifacts, and ask the
  founder through the direct work loop.
- Any outward effect parks at the wall. No model-authored content or configuration can mint release
  authority.
- A product bet works in an isolated git worktree, retains its exact difference, and requires
  founder review plus explicit local apply. Deploy keeps a second explicit authorization.
- Only the founder ends a bet; a killed line may seed a learned fork but never auto-ends.
- A bet at the wall remains inspectable, discussable, and forkable without mutating the exact staged
  effect awaiting a decision.

## 6. The wall

- One surface distinguishes outward release, teammate answer, outcome review, and bet ending.
- Each item admits only decisions valid for its purpose and leaves a durable receipt.
- Away holds every outward act while inward work continues.
- Browser, API, MCP, runtime, stored content, and configuration cannot self-approve or forge founder
  presence. Batch review never becomes delegated authority.

## 7. Firm configuration

- The founder can inspect and change one readable, versioned venture configuration through natural
  language or direct manipulation.
- Proposals show human-readable before/after differences, apply only by founder action, and can be
  restored as a new attributable version.
- Presentation, organization, runtime, capability lenses, context, memory, coordination, activation,
  evaluation, budgets, and restrictive authority remain configuration—not a second actor or work
  ontology.
- Configuration can narrow behavior but cannot expand host authority.
- Closed choices exist only for finite adapter and safety mechanics. They cannot become role, bet,
  workflow, or market taxonomies.

## 8. Outcomes, taste, and continuation

- Provider identity deduplicates returned records; evidence joins an outcome to the provoking bet or
  leaves it honestly unattributed.
- Positive, negative, and zero outcomes render as the market speaking, without sentiment scores or
  aggregate founder-facing scoreboards.
- Founder decisions and joined outcomes feed later teammate judgment with attribution.
- A real reply alerts the founder for a joint decision and can change the next fork.

## 9. Portfolio and transfer

- One portfolio-wide founder wall can show decisions from at least two isolated ventures and route
  each decision to its owner; conversation, configuration, canvas, heat, spend, bets, and outcomes
  remain scoped to one selected venture.
- Export/import preserves readable configuration, conversation, bets, outcomes, decisions, heat,
  placement, and portable product-change history while stripping machine-local bindings.
- Imported work does not resume until the founder explicitly binds a valid destination repository.

## 10. Desktop experience

- The production target is desktop only. Verify the full founder journey at 1440×900 and 1280×800;
  phone and tablet layouts are not acceptance criteria.
- Keyboard focus, selection, wall decisions, configuration review, and composer use remain complete;
  visible focus, semantic names, contrast, and reduced motion meet the WCAG 2.2 AA target.
- The canvas/conversation relationship, wall, selected scope, errors, loading, and empty states stay
  understandable without relying on color alone or clipping primary controls.

## Deterministic release gate

```sh
npm run test:acceptance
```

The command must pass brain tests, interface unit tests, lint, production build, design-token parity,
the four preserved operating journeys, and the three Living Venture Atlas journeys. Record exact
counts and visual evidence in `STATE.md` only after rerunning them on the current tree.

## Alpha proof gate

The company bet remains unproven until an outside founder binds a real product, directs useful work,
releases an exact outward act, receives an attributable positive, negative, or zero outcome, and can
choose the next move without intervention. Deterministic fixtures cannot satisfy this gate.
