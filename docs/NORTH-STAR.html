The shape

Spiky — product-real, market-paper.

The strategy core (worth-building, capability, IA) and the loop (taste) are climbing toward Cursor-level and are largely real. The four market layers (positioning presence, founder-fit, distribution, desire) are frontier-sound but at zero external reality. A round grade would hide the hole. The honest read is one fatal gap that gates four layers at once: no real outside human has closed a loop. Everything routes to tripping that.

The plan — two tracks, joined at the gate

Track A — finish the product half

  1. Measure becomes real (capture the motion's real outcome)
  2. The gated send — one click, still behind the wall
  3. Prove taste compounds visibly (one approval + rerun)
  4. The project-aware one-prompt entry
  5. Adaptive-per-motion surface + craft pass

Track B — open the market half

  1. Publish the category — one citable artifact a model can name
  2. Eat your own dog food — point GTM IDE at itself
  3. Build in public — the closed loop is the post
  4. Put it in front of one real dev-tool founder
Convergence: Track A gets just far enough that an outside founder can close a loop unaided; Track B puts it in front of one. That single event — a stranger closes a loop and gets a real outcome — flips four layers from paper to real at once. That is the moonshot, and it has a date: 30 days.
Crucible eval · GTM IDE · craft layer de-scoped (founder owns UI craft). The eval is the asset that compounds; the build is downstream. The bar rises from here.
