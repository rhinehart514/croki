The base is The Living Map — but steal the Machine's pulsing run and the Calm Board's restraint.

The Living Map is the truest to everything we decided (one canvas, the current, living nodes). It just needs the Machine's "it visibly runs" moment so the harness reads as alive, and the Calm Board's discipline so 5 channels stay calm, not busy. The Team and Mission Control are *modes* you could zoom into, not the home. The Stream is the fallback if the spatial map ever feels like too much.
