1

Position is status. The column a card sits in is its state — no chips, badges, or progress UI anywhere. The only thing that can happen to a card is moving right.

2

Band is territory. Product above, GTM below, one hairline. A card is Product or GTM by where it sits; crossings are vertical edges between bands.

3

Dashed is provisional. Agent-made cards and drafted crossings use dashed strokes with transparent fill; adopted truth is solid with panel fill. Adoption solidifies the stroke in place, 150ms, no ceremony. Selection is the blue ring; the composer gains the scope chip.

4

The push is incomplete ink. An open question is one amber line on its card; a missing crossing is a dashed amber edge. Nothing floats, nothing is modal, nothing nags in chat. Selecting the card puts its question in the composer placeholder.

5

Evidence returns at the right edge and curves back only along existing joins — to the release it came from and the product truth it touches. Blue, dashed, quiet. Its interpretation stays unresolved until adopted.

6

The composer is the only input. No drag, no palette, no node drawing. Click selects and scopes; Enter expands the card in place (citation, linked Thread, adopt / prune); arrow keys walk the graph along edges; Esc clears scope.

7

The camera never moves on its own. New cards fade in at their column in 200ms; off-screen arrivals surface as this pill, which pans on click. Work state (the blue dot) is derived from the linked Thread, never stored on the graph.
