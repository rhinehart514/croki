Drover · Product / GTM section

Five shapes for talking a venture into clarity

Fixed lines: everything reads left to right — thought → committed → building → in market → evidence — across two lanes, Product and GTM, joined by crossings. The founder never draws. The freedom is conversational; Drover pushes for clarity and movement. These five differ in one thing only: where the agent’s hands are, and where yours are.

01

The map takes dictation

You talk; the map is the transcript’s shadow, updating live. Nodes slide in mid-sentence, crossings draw themselves, a misfiled thought migrates lanes when you correct it.

Push
Questions are objects, not chat. The unanswered distribution question hangs off the node as a visibly incomplete edge — the loudest thing on screen.
Breaks
Misplacement. One wrong filing erodes trust in the whole wall. Live ink must read as provisional, or it collides with adopted truth.
02

Everything is a proposal

The live map never mutates under you. Each riff returns a staged revision shown as a diff — ghost nodes, ghost crossings, slide-trails — adopted in one act. A PR against your product map.

Push
Baked into every proposal: your idea, plus its crossing, plus “these two have stalled nine days — here’s the move.”
Breaks
Fast movement. A turnstile inside ideation breeds rubber-stamping, and then the ceremony is theater. Right mechanic, wrong scope.
03

The clarity ratchet

Rightward movement is gated by named questions, and the interface leads with the question. One card in focus — the highest-leverage unanswered question on the board. Answer or park; the node advances; the next loads.

Push
It is the interface. Every session ends with things measurably further right.
Breaks
Flirts with being a wizard. If question-picking is mediocre, it’s a form with delusions. Free talk must override at every moment. Converges; doesn’t diverge.
04

The board that worked overnight

One standing, revocable, inward-only direction: keep working the map. You return to what moved — grounded branches, drafted crossings, plays parked at the gate — and spend the session on pure judgment.

Push
The map advanced without you and now needs you. The pull to return is “my staff has been working.”
Breaks
Authority. Ambient work was deliberately killed; this needs a new founder-granted standing contract, shaped like the Gmail observation grant. A spec decision, not a UX detail.
05

The corridor

Default view is one node in focus with only its immediate left and right visible. Committing the next step slides the corridor. Pinch out for the wall; working state is one node deep.

Push
Spatialized as emptiness ahead — the right of the corridor stays blank until the node earns its next step.
Breaks
Sacrifices the wall — the at-a-glance clarity that is the point. A zoom state, not a paradigm.

The call

Base the section on 01 — dictation. It alone delivers the named feeling: total conversational autonomy inside fixed lines.

The rest are organs, not competitors. 02 lives only at the adoption boundary — live ink stays visibly provisional; adopting reviews the delta. 03 is how the push surfaces after each founder turn: one sharp question, skippable, never modal. 05 is the double-click zoom on any node. 04 is the endgame, built only after the dictation loop proves the map changes what gets built.

Riskiest interaction: dictation’s structuring accuracy — whether the agent files a riff in the right lane, at the right position, often enough to trust the wall. Prototype that first, on real venture data, before any visual polish.
