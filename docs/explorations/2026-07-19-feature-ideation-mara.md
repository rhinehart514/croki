# Feature ideation — 2026-07-19 (Mara)

Exploration only. Evidence until an authority file adopts it (per AGENTS.md).

Grounding: docs/STATE.md L1-L80 — the three-mode shell passes the full acceptance
gate deterministically, but the tree does not prove outside-founder comprehension,
market value, or an external consequence. DESIGN.md L1-L60 — the release, not a
thread or mode, is what moves the venture. Ideation therefore targets the seams
where inward proof should become outward consequence.

## 1. Evidence Return Inbox (Releases)

Capture what the market sends back after an outward action — a reply, install,
star, signup, or explicit silence past a date — and hold it beside its release,
unresolved, until the founder assigns the consequence: changes Product, changes
GTM, or changes nothing (with a reason). A Product consequence opens scoped
direction in Work; a GTM consequence lands on the canvas. Makes the fifth leg of
the Release Path generative instead of decorative. Bet
bet-20260720033720-565bd403; draft staged-20260720033735-d50c89da.

## 2. Unreleased Delta Ledger (Releases)

When meaningful work merges in Work mode, project it into Releases as an unlinked
Product delta carrying its one-line distribution question: who outside this
workspace should feel this change, and through what door? Founder links it to a
release, starts a draft from it, or marks it internal-only with a reason. Pure
projection from WorkIndex — no workflow object, no status field. Bet
bet-20260720033723-4a5c3cdb; draft staged-20260720033741-15737095.

## 3. Attempt Racing (Work)

Point Claude and Codex at the same direction in parallel isolated worktrees,
compare their diffs side by side in the existing DiffView, apply the winner
exactly; the loser stays as inspectable evidence on the thread. Builds on proven
PTY worktree isolation and the in-flight DiffView/WorkChangesPane work. Bet
bet-20260720033725-1b5bb679; draft staged-20260720033747-e7e9b4b0.

## Recommendation

Evidence Return Inbox first: it is the only one that closes the loop the approved
direction names as the product's identity (evidence-returning system), and it is
the cheapest to prove with one real release.

Note: the staged venture architecture is the Buffalo Projects fixture while the
venture repository is the Drover tree; this ideation follows the repository truth.
