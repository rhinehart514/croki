# VISION — Croki

## Thesis

Speed is a moat. The founder who can change the Product and every path to market, learn from reality, and make
the next move before competitors coordinate has a structural advantage.

Croki is a Product and GTM Development Environment for founders building with agents. It gives one founder a
coherent environment for directing Claude and Codex, pursuing many approaches, doing work that does not scale,
changing the Product when reality demands it, and compounding every useful result into improving velocity.

## Destination

One founder can hold PLG, outbound, inbound, partnerships, founder-led sales, manual onboarding, bespoke work,
and unusual experiments at once without forcing them into one universal container. Agents pursue ambitious exact
work across those approaches while current Product truth remains clear.

Product interpretation is plural and durable. Product authority is singular. Agents can create competing
source-bearing models and revise them over time; only the founder selectively makes changes current.

Every world crossing keeps its real identity and stops at an exact founder decision. Returned evidence travels
back to the Product/GTM claims it can actually change. The next work begins with that accumulated context.

## The compounding loop

```text
direction → exact work → provisional Product/GTM alternatives
          → founder-gated outward action → evidence
          → selective Product change → faster next work
```

Croki wins when each loop is faster and more informed than the one before it—not when it contains more workflow
machinery.

## Non-goals

Croki is not an AI org chart, pipeline builder, release manager, campaign database, autonomous CEO, or safety
dashboard. It does not reduce Claude or Codex to a planner. It does not require scalable process before learning.
It does not turn every market act into a release or every approach into a campaign.

## Proof standard

The product is coherent only when a real founder completes the full loop with a real repository, real provider,
real outward action, attributable evidence, selective Product-model merge, and a demonstrably faster next move.
Screenshots and mechanical tests are necessary but insufficient.
