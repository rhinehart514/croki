# VISION — Drover

Logged 2026-07-03. Revised 2026-07-10 for the terrain-first product direction.

This is the north star. [STATE.md](STATE.md) records what the current build and evaluations actually
prove; `AGENTS.md` records the system's engineering constraints.

## The one sentence

**Drover reads your product, shows where it may have leverage, gives you a crew to work the
uncertainty, turns your choice into a safe go-to-market move, and brings the market's answer back to
what you build.**

## The product you experience

You point Drover at a product. Before asking you to invent a goal, it opens a living product-market
terrain. You can see what the code proves, what Drover thinks may matter, where the story or product is
under strain, and what evidence would change those reads.

The difference between fact and judgment is always visible. Product truth carries a receipt. A possible
opening is labeled as an inference or bet. Missing market evidence stays missing instead of being filled
with confident copy.

You choose what deserves attention. Your persistent crew can disagree, investigate, and show its
reasoning without forcing you through a questionnaire. When you choose a move, Drover turns that part of
the terrain into an editable pipeline, runs it to your wall, and returns the outcome to the same terrain.
The next read starts from what the market actually taught.

## The mental model

The **terrain** is primary. It is the durable, evolving picture of the product meeting its market:
cited product truth, founder-stated facts, possible openings and tensions, unresolved questions, chosen
moves, decisions, and outcomes. It is useful before the first pipeline exists.

The **operation** is the worked layer. It shows where attention is active now: what the crew is exploring,
which questions remain open, which moves are running, what waits for founder judgment, and what has come
back.

A **pipeline** is one chosen move. It is not the whole product and it is not required before Drover can
help. It is the open execution path for making one decision real.

Operator is where you understand the whole terrain and decide where to work. Engineer is where you inspect
and shape one selected pipeline. These are two views of the same product, not separate products.

## What makes Drover compound

1. **Grounded understanding.** A read-only scan builds cited product truth without an AI call. Drover can
   therefore prove what it understood and remain useful when no model is connected.

2. **Rented judgment.** Codex or Claude Code can supply research, interpretation, composition, and drafting
   through the founder's local subscription. The model is replaceable; product truth, decisions, outcomes,
   and safety do not belong to one provider.

3. **Comprehension before action.** Drover reveals the credible field without hiding the uncertainty or
   forcing one recommended answer. A founder can inspect, correct, ask, investigate, or act directly.

4. **A persistent crew.** Teammates remain attached to the product across moves. Each teammate keeps one
   deterministic illustrated character everywhere it appears, alongside useful lessons, distinct positions,
   and a record of how its work performed. The crew does not restart as anonymous chat turns or initials.

5. **Market return.** A release is not a result. Only an observed, honestly attributed outcome can teach the
   terrain, the next pipeline, the crew, and what the founder may build next.

## The invariants

The host constrains only three things:

- **Truth.** Existing product claims are cited or labeled as inference.
- **The founder wall.** Nothing sends, publishes, deploys, or charges without founder authorization. The
  wall can graduate through explicit trust, but it never disappears.
- **Taste.** Founder approvals, rejections, and edits become durable memory that shapes later work.

Everything else that requires judgment belongs to rented intelligence. Drover must not freeze strategy into
a host-side ranking engine, require a question or plan before useful work, impose a fixed go-to-market stage
sequence, or turn terrain hypotheses into authority.

## The alpha test

The software is not the proof. The proof is a founder opening a real product, understanding a credible area
of leverage, choosing and running a move through the wall, observing a real market response with honest
lineage, and seeing that learning change the next terrain and next work.

Alpha ends only when an outside founder can survive that loop without explanation. A green deterministic
suite, a polished fixture, or a successful model call does not substitute for it.
