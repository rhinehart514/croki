# Mission and Product Contract

## Mission

Drover is a persistent AI GTM team embedded in the AI coding experience.

The crew works across the full product-development relationship with the market:

```text
Market reality
  ↕
GTM understanding
  ↕
Product decision
  ↕
Code or product change
  ↕
Distribution, activation, or proof
  ↕
Market response
```

This is still GTM. Drover is not becoming a generic product-management tool.

## User promise

For a founder building from a codebase, Drover should make it easier to:

- understand who the product is for and why it matters;
- distinguish product truth from market belief;
- see what the current GTM crew believes and where it disagrees;
- choose what to build, test, expose, or distribute next;
- turn product changes into GTM-native actions;
- connect market outcomes back to future product and coding decisions;
- preserve founder judgment without forcing the founder to perform every mechanical step.

## 100x wedge

The non-table-stakes opportunity is not AI-generated copy, an MCP bridge, or a pipeline dashboard.

The wedge is continuity:

> A product change, a GTM action, a market response, and the next code decision live in one durable,
> evidence-backed loop, worked by a persistent GTM crew.

The most valuable GTM actions are often product changes themselves:

- an in-product referral loop;
- a product-generated public page;
- a proof surface;
- an activation improvement;
- a new instrumentation point;
- a calculator, diagnostic, or microproduct cut from real product logic;
- a product surface designed to be discovered or shared;
- a capability shaped around a real buyer trigger.

## Product contracts

1. Every product claim is grounded, founder-stated, inferred, or speculative.
2. Every teammate has a distinct GTM point of view and durable product-specific memory.
3. Every founder decision is visible, durable, and able to shape future work.
4. Every external or irreversible action remains behind the founder gate.
5. Every meaningful market outcome can return to the product and the crew.
6. No pre-run object or contract blocks a freely composed action before the gate.
7. The model owns fuzzy GTM judgment; the host owns truth, wall, taste, persistence, and typed mutations.
8. The system must remain useful when intelligence is unavailable: it reports an honest blank or refusal.

## Non-goals

- Do not become a CRM.
- Do not become a generic product analytics suite.
- Do not become an agent marketplace.
- Do not become a fixed campaign-template library.
- Do not replace the coding agent.
- Do not create a hosted strategy engine that hardcodes GTM judgment.
- Do not require a question, program, policy, or measurement object before any action can reach the gate.

## Production definition

This is a complete production direction, not a prototype. Production means the chosen records are durable,
the UI and MCP surfaces share semantics, the full loop handles empty/loading/error/partial states, and a
founder can complete the code → GTM → market → product loop without hidden manual reconciliation.
