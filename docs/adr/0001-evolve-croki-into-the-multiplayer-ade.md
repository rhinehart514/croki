# Evolve Croki into the multiplayer ADE

Croki will evolve directly from Jacob's personal agentic development environment into a multiplayer ADE for small software product teams. Jacob's daily work remains the proving ground, and the existing provider-native development loop remains the product foundation; we will not fork a separately named team product or generalize into non-software work before teams prove that a teammate can enter long-running agent work midway, understand reality without a private briefing, intervene usefully, and help it reach a verified result.
