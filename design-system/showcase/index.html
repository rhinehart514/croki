@gtm-ide/design-system

Welcome to the kit..

A soft, airy, monochrome system for composing go-to-market workflows. Frosted glass surfaces, pill controls, one ink ramp — 12 components and 66 tokens, distraction-free.

Source ICP accounts from GitHub… Enrich & score a prospect list… Draft grounded openers…
Compose a go-to-market workflow — background soft pastel, surfaces brushed glass, thin modern icons. The graph should feel airy and distraction-free
Graph Run
Sonnet 4.5
Principles
01

Soft & airy

A warm greige canvas, a faint dot grid, and frosted-glass surfaces. Generous space; nothing competes for attention.

02

Monochrome ink

One zinc ramp carries hierarchy through weight and presence. Color appears only to signal state or node category.

03

Pill & glass

Controls are pills, surfaces are glass, the primary action is a single near-black ink button. Calm reads as confidence.

Foundations

Tokens, not hexes

Every value below is a CSS custom property declared once on :root. Layout glue references them; nothing is hardcoded.
