# Croki product contract

`docs/FIRM-SPEC.md` is the durable authority. This file is its compact product translation.

## Promise

Croki is the low-latency, cross-project agentic development environment for founders directing native
Claude and Codex.

Work is the default and hot path: enter a project, resume exact context, direct the selected SDK, and
review or apply its work. Product / GTM is each project's causal coherence layer, compounding direct
work, market movement, and evidence into faster next moves without becoming a gate or alternate hidden
participant.

## Outcome

The founder moves between projects and exact agent work with first-party-client immediacy, while
Product and GTM remain coherent enough to learn from reality without losing current truth, provenance,
or authority over what crosses into the world.

## Experience

- First run connects a real repository, opens Work, exposes Claude or Codex, and asks: **What are you
  trying to make true?** Work begins before grounding or workflow configuration.
- Work is the default direct Claude/Codex agentic development environment: conversation, isolated
  attempts, code, terminal, preview, tests, artifacts, evidence, and exact review.
- Work is one loop—return, direct, watch, correct, verify, ship, return—with only Project, Thread, and
  the exact Review as persistent founder-visible anchors. Review is a contextual projection, not
  another work container; attempts, agents, tools, and shipping become prominent only when exact work
  requires them.
- Product / GTM is the project-scoped coherence and consequence layer over the living venture model:
  it shares Work's T3-shaped shell and exact Threads while keeping a spatial center. It opens on the
  code-proven page walk, then contextually recomposes around a Product consequence, full GTM play, or
  evidence return. Page refinement mints an exact Work Thread; an adopted Product consequence earns
  the action that joins or drafts a play. Source-preserving aggregate journey evidence may overlay the
  map without becoming Product truth.
- Return and project switching restore the exact last-coherent Thread or Product/GTM selection rather
  than asking the founder to reconstruct context.

## Product model

Each project has one isolated venture store containing one adopted Product/GTM model and multiple
durable provisional branches. Agents may revise source-bearing alternatives. Only the founder
selectively makes changes current.

One broad direction may grant a durable, revocable scope for continuing safe inward work. It may survive
restart and wake on attributable evidence. It never grants outward action, destructive work, capability
expansion, or truth promotion.

A release is a Product release. Interviews, outreach, manual onboarding, partner actions, deployments,
publications, spend, and bespoke work keep their real identity as outward actions.

## Vocabulary

Live work is derived. Motion means a broad growth/value route. Branch means a Product/GTM alternative.
Mechanism is earned reusable capacity. Campaign is optional and bounded. Pipeline is literal-only.
Thread is durable direction. Run is one exact attempt. There is no universal work-container noun.

## Invariants

- Product and GTM may both change substantially.
- Unscalable work is first-class.
- Work is the default and hot path; Product/GTM grounding never blocks project entry, resume, provider
  dispatch, or direct Work controls.
- The founder-selected Claude or Codex SDK remains the Work participant. Product / GTM never silently
  replaces it; specialists require an exact, visible invocation.
- Claude and Codex retain native capability.
- Current truth is singular; interpretation may be plural.
- Agent claims remain tentative and source-bearing.
- Founder authority is exact and consequence-based.
- Venture data, credentials, repositories, worktrees, and evidence never cross venture scope.
- Work and Product / GTM are the only founder surfaces.
- The Product/GTM canvas is the signature coherence surface; current and provisional truth cannot be
  confused.
- Product/GTM uses the same project shell, selected Claude/Codex participant, Thread identity, composer
  continuity, density, and interaction grammar as Work; it does not become another coding pane.
- The Product walk is the resting projection. Automatic composition, not founder organization, makes
  focused consequences, plays, and evidence paths readable.
- Repository facts and adopted aggregate observations refresh automatically. Product consequences,
  causal explanations, joins, and plays remain provisional until founder adoption.
- Intended desktop acceptance targets are p95 `100ms` acknowledgement for local interaction, p95
  `250ms` usable content for a warm project or Thread switch, and founder-turn display by the next
  paint with a durable Thread/Run address within p95 `500ms`. Provider startup is measured separately.
- Switching, partial data, offline state, and failed resume preserve input, exact address, and the last
  coherent content instead of presenting an empty shell.
- Compatibility is read-only and never dictates current UI or language.
