# Drover product contract

`docs/FIRM-SPEC.md` is the durable authority. This file is its compact product translation.

## Promise

Drover is a Product and GTM Development Environment for founders building with agents.

It lets one founder run Product and go-to-market as one fast, changing system: direct Claude and Codex,
pursue several routes to growth, change the Product when reality demands it, and compound useful
results into faster next moves.

## Outcome

The founder moves faster than the market can react without losing coherence, current truth, exact work,
or authority over what crosses into the world.

## Experience

- First run connects a real repository, confirms Claude or Codex, and asks: **What are you trying to
  make true?** Work begins before workflow configuration.
- Work is the direct Claude/Codex agentic development environment: conversation, isolated attempts,
  code, terminal, preview, tests, artifacts, evidence, and exact review.
- Product / GTM is the founder's intent layer over the living venture model: the product mapped as
  the pages a user walks through (left to right, connections only the code proves) with click-to-refine
  that mints exact Work Threads; GTM plays as workflows in drafted and established registers; plus
  current truth, durable provisional alternatives, attached work, outward actions, evidence return,
  and in-place selective merge.
- Return restores exact context and makes changed, moving, failed, stale, returned, and founder-only
  decisions visible through their owning Threads and paths.

## Product model

One venture store contains one adopted Product/GTM model and multiple durable provisional branches.
Agents may revise source-bearing alternatives. Only the founder selectively makes changes current.

One broad direction may grant a durable, revocable scope for continuing safe inward work. It may survive
restart and wake on attributable evidence. It never grants outward action, destructive work, capability
expansion, or truth promotion.

A release is a Product release. Interviews, outreach, manual onboarding, partner actions, deployments,
publications, spend, and bespoke work keep their real identity as outward actions.

## Vocabulary

Live work is derived. Motion means a broad growth/value route. Branch means a Product/GTM alternative.
Mechanism is earned reusable capacity. Campaign is optional and bounded. Pipeline is literal-only.
Thread is durable direction. Run is one exact attempt. There is no universal work-container noun.

## Invariants

- Product and GTM may both change substantially.
- Unscalable work is first-class.
- Claude and Codex retain native capability.
- Current truth is singular; interpretation may be plural.
- Agent claims remain tentative and source-bearing.
- Founder authority is exact and consequence-based.
- Venture data, credentials, repositories, worktrees, and evidence never cross venture scope.
- Work and Product / GTM are the only founder surfaces.
- The canvas is the signature surface; current and provisional truth cannot be confused.
- Compatibility is read-only and never dictates current UI or language.
