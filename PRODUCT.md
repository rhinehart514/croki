# Drover product contract

**Authority:** [docs/FIRM-SPEC.md](docs/FIRM-SPEC.md) is the spec of record.
[docs/STATE.md](docs/STATE.md) records what is built and what remains unproven. This file is the
compact product and UX translation; it cannot override either.

## Purpose

Drover is the living venture atlas for one-person holding companies, entered through **vibe code your
go-to-market**. On one continuous canvas, the founder shapes how each venture creates value and
reaches the market, then directs persistent teammates through that architecture. Drover keeps inward
work moving and holds every world-touching effect for the founder.

The first proof is one real venture. The destination is many transferable ventures behind one
founder-controlled boundary, with only founder-blessed patterns crossing between them. Conversation, configuration,
architecture, canvas placement, heat, spend, work, and returned evidence are scoped to the selected venture;
founder decisions share one portfolio-wide authority surface. “Whole firm” inside a venture means that venture's
configured participants, never an implicit merge of venture data.

## Founder language

Drover does not teach the founder an operating taxonomy. It uses the founder's words and concrete
descriptions of the work: who this helps, how they get value, how to reach them, what is changing,
what is ready to go out, and what happened afterward. Labels should usually be short verb phrases,
real artifacts, audiences, or recognizable business language—not names for Drover containers.

Internal reasoning may distinguish reusable capabilities, ways to reach people, bounded campaigns,
uncertain claims, branches of work, releases, and returned evidence. Those distinctions protect
reuse, attribution, and authority; they are not a request to expose **system**, **motion**, **bet**,
**fork**, **outcome**, **the wall**, **pipeline**, **stage**, or **work item** as required founder vocabulary. A
founder who uses one of those words keeps it. Otherwise the interface says what is actually happening.

Historical identifiers such as `bet`, `fork`, `gtm-ide`, and `channel` remain valid compatibility
seams until an intentional migration. New founder copy must not leak them. Desired futures and
generated deliverables are never described as returned reality.

## Core experience

The desktop workbench keeps one persistent firm conversation beside a live infinite canvas. With no
selection, the founder addresses the whole venture. Selecting any visible part of the venture, teammate,
campaign, or exact workpiece narrows the same conversation and composer; it does not open another workflow
or chat product.

The canvas is the product surface and a lens over venture records, never a second database. It remains
a continuous infinite spatial world with pan, zoom, direct placement, stable landmarks, drag
relationships, camera history, and focus that expands in place. Far away it shows venture intent,
how product value happens, the active ways of reaching people, current campaigns, work nearing a
founder decision, returned reality, and founder judgment. Deeper views reveal the founder's own
ideas, named areas, reusable capabilities, and shared paths. Near exact work it exposes evidence,
prepared differences, lineage, returned evidence, machinery
receipts, and decisions. Placement owns coordinates and camera only; semantic architecture remains
durable venture truth.

Creation is open first. Jacob may type, place, group, and connect ideas without choosing a type.
One sentence may also produce an immediate working theory of the whole venture and begin useful
inward work. Inferred structure is visibly provisional: it can organize the canvas and guide work,
but it does not become durable architecture, claim evidence, or gain authority merely because the
model drew it. Founder edits are immediate and undoable; the work itself supplies evidence for what
the lasting structure should become.

The operating loop in plain language is **understand → do safe work → ask before crossing into the
world → see what happened → adapt**. One heat dial and spend rail govern how hard inward work runs.
Heat never weakens founder authority.

An approval holds the exact effect under review, not the founder's access to the surrounding work.
Held work remains inspectable and discussable, and the founder may ask for another approach without
mutating the exact effect awaiting a decision.

## Product invariants

1. One plain-language direction immediately yields a coherent, editable working theory and at least
   one useful inward action. The founder never has to approve an empty plan before value appears.
2. Founder surfaces use the founder's words or concrete business language. No internal noun is
   required to understand, direct, inspect, or stop work.
3. Inferred structure is unmistakably provisional and revises as work and evidence return. It may
   guide safe inward work but cannot silently become durable truth or authority.
4. Every visible grouping is anchored to concrete work, an artifact, evidence, or a real relationship.
   Drover never materializes empty containers merely to make the venture look complete.
5. Nothing sends, publishes, deploys, or spends without the founder's explicit hand.
6. Only the founder ends active work.
7. The founder can inspect and refine anything without passing through a workflow gate.
8. Claims about the bound product cite repository evidence or are labeled inference.
9. Ventures never bleed; only founder-blessed lessons cross as patterns.
10. Firm configuration is readable, versioned, attributable, and reversible. It may narrow behavior
   but cannot create host authority.
11. Domain vocabulary stays open. Closed values are allowed only for finite adapter or safety
   mechanics such as available runtimes, activation behavior, and outward authority; they never
   classify people, work, or what the market may say.
12. Returned reality stays language and evidence, not sentiment scores or aggregate scoreboards.
13. The venture remains local-first, exportable, and independent of provider-owned durable state.
14. Open canvas relationships have no hidden execution meaning; operational meaning is explicit.
15. Deleting placement loses no architecture, evidence, history, or execution context.
16. Selecting something must change teammate context and return priority or the atlas has failed.

## Experience character

Grounded, consequential, and calm. Drover should feel like a capable firm under the founder's hand,
not a generic AI chat wrapper, workflow builder, CRM board, template dashboard, synthetic org-chart
simulator, or page containing a diagram. Persistent identity, causal evidence, exact differences,
spatial continuity, and visible receipts create trust. Motion is purposeful and polished: camera
focus, architecture promotion, branching work, release, return, and accepted architecture change may animate
once to explain consequence. Decorative work simulation, looping motion, fake metrics, jargon, and
machinery do not create sophistication.

## Platform and accessibility

Drover is a desktop founder workbench. Phone and tablet layouts are intentionally out of scope.
Target WCAG 2.2 AA: every founder action is keyboard reachable with visible focus; status never
relies on color alone; text and controls meet contrast requirements; reduced motion removes
nonessential animation; loading, empty, error, and zoomed-canvas states remain understandable.
