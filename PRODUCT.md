# Drover product contract

## Authority and status

[docs/FIRM-SPEC.md](docs/FIRM-SPEC.md) defines Drover's durable product and build physics.
[docs/STATE.md](docs/STATE.md) records what the current tree proves.
[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) defines the intended desktop experience. This file is a compact target
contract and overrides none of them.

## Product promise

Drover is the operating system for one-person holding companies, entered through **vibe code your
go-to-market**. A founder directs a persistent firm through one conversation and a living venture
atlas that makes product work, market work, decisions, and returned evidence visible together.

One real venture is the first proof. The destination is a portfolio of isolated, transferable
ventures behind one founder-controlled boundary. Only founder-approved patterns cross between
ventures; conversation, work, evidence, configuration, and spend remain scoped to the selected
venture.

## Founder experience

One ordinary direction, such as “help this venture grow,” should immediately create a coherent,
editable working theory and begin useful inward work. That theory is visibly provisional and changes
as the founder edits it and real evidence returns. The founder never has to approve an empty plan or
administer an internal taxonomy before value appears.

The desktop workbench keeps one durable venture conversation beside a continuous spatial canvas.
Selecting a venture, teammate, campaign, or exact piece of work scopes that same conversation and
composer. It does not open a separate chat product or workflow.

The canvas is a lens over durable venture records, not a second database. It exposes how value is
created, how people may be reached, what work is underway, what awaits the founder, and what happened
afterward. Placement and camera state may change without changing semantic architecture, evidence,
history, or execution context.

The operating loop is simple: **understand → do safe work → ask before touching the world → see what
happened → adapt**. Heat and spend govern how hard inward work runs; neither can weaken founder
authority.

## Durable invariants

1. Founder surfaces use the founder's words and concrete business language. Internal identifiers and
   record types never become required vocabulary.
2. Inferred structure remains unmistakably provisional. It may guide safe inward work but cannot
   silently become durable truth, evidence, or authority.
3. Every visible grouping is anchored to real work, an artifact, evidence, or a real relationship.
   Drover does not create empty containers to make a venture look complete.
4. Nothing sends, publishes, deploys, or spends without the founder's explicit hand. Only the founder
   ends active work.
5. Claims about the bound product cite repository evidence or are labeled inference. Returned reality
   keeps its language, source, attribution, and uncertainty.
6. Ventures never bleed. Configuration is readable, versioned, attributable, and reversible, but it
   cannot create host or outward authority.
7. Domain vocabulary stays open. Closed values are reserved for finite adapter and safety mechanics,
   never for classifying people, work, or what the market may say.
8. Open canvas relationships have no hidden execution meaning. Selecting something must change
   teammate context and return priority, or the atlas has failed.
9. Venture state remains local-first, exportable, and independent of provider-owned durable state.

## Product character and platform

Drover should feel like a capable firm under the founder's hand: grounded, consequential, spatial,
and calm. Trust comes from persistent identity, causal evidence, exact differences, and visible
receipts, not activity theater, fake metrics, decorative motion, or jargon.

The shipped founder product is an Electron desktop workbench. Phone and tablet layouts are out of
scope. The browser build is a development and deterministic-test harness, not a second production
surface. Accessibility and visual behavior follow [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
